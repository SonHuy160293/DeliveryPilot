## Authority and evidence labels

- **user-provided:** The product owner's direction, including D1–D6. It is not evidence of customer prevalence or usability.
- **repository-verified:** A fact about the documents reviewed. Intended product behavior does not prove implementation.
- **externally-sourced:** An attributed observation or recommendation with source limitations.
- **AI-inference:** A proposed persona, interpretation, priority, workflow, state model or design hypothesis.

The PH01–PH07 codes in the earlier research denote an engagement-wide journey. PH01 presales and PH02 contracting remain upstream context; the product starts with their signed outputs. LC01–LC08 denote the current product lifecycle. Native invoice issuing/accounting is excluded initially; payment conditions and external evidence references remain relevant to delivery decisions.

PP pain IDs, N need IDs, FP rules and O opportunity IDs are local research references. They do not allocate canonical UR/FR/US IDs. Feature IDs link to the roadmap; this pack does not promote feature status or define implementation tasks.
