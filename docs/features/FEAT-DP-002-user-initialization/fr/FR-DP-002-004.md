# FR-DP-002-004 — Reject invalid authentication attempts safely

## Requirement statement
The system shall reject invalid registration/authentication attempts and dependency failures without disclosing credentials or granting access as a fallback.

## Preconditions and trigger
Registration or login fails validation/proof, encounters a service failure, or meets a reviewed abuse-control condition.

## Main behavior
Give safe actionable validation/failure feedback, preserve existing identity ownership, and never treat failure as authentication. Registration abuse limits, lockout and durable security-event retention are deferred for this phase.

## Validation, business rules and authorization
NFR-002 and NFR-008 apply. Invalid fields and failed proof never grant identity/resource authority. Duplicate registration feedback follows the user-confirmed FR-DP-002-002 contract. Do not introduce Redis/rate limiting or lockout while implementing the initial registration phase.

## Failure and edge cases
Cover invalid input, dependency failure and ambiguous retries. A future abuse-control policy needs separate refinement; it is not a prerequisite to the current registration deliverable.

## Verification criteria
- Invalid/rejected requests produce no new authenticated access or usable partial registration.
- Failures do not reveal raw secrets or alter another account.
- No abuse-control outage behavior or numeric limiter requirement is introduced in this phase.

## Open questions
No blocker for initial registration. Future abuse controls and account-recovery behavior remain deferred.
