# US-DP-002-001 — Register a platform identity

## Story
As a prospective platform user, I want to establish my platform identity so that I can begin the permitted onboarding journey.

## Context and boundaries
Confirmed phase: anonymous DisplayName/Email/Password registration, platform-wide case-insensitive email uniqueness, immediate active status, no email verification and no automatic login. JWT issuance belongs to login. Abuse/lockout and durable security-evidence work are deferred; confidentiality is mandatory.

## Acceptance criteria
### AC-001 — Valid registration
**Given** valid DisplayName, Email and Password for an unregistered email, **When** an anonymous applicant registers, **Then** one active identity and protected credential commit together and Created/userId is returned, without verification or automatic login.

### AC-002 — Invalid input
**Given** a missing/invalid required field, **When** registration is submitted, **Then** validation fails before registration commits and no usable partial identity remains.

### AC-003 — Duplicate and concurrent submission
**Given** trim/case-equivalent emails, **When** registration is repeated or concurrent, **Then** at most one registration unit commits and duplicates report "This email is exist" without changing the original account or exposing its userId.

### AC-004 — No implicit authority
**Given** a new platform identity without resource grants, **When** it is created or a caller supplies role/tenant identifiers, **Then** registration grants no tenant/project/approver access or authenticated session.

### AC-005 — Failure confidentiality
**Given** hashing, staging or commit fails, **When** registration is attempted, **Then** no success, partial usable registration or authenticated access results and diagnostics do not expose raw passwords.

## Dependencies and testing notes
Atomic persistence, uniqueness and typed collision handling must support the transport and protected-hashing flow. Test PostgreSQL concurrency, rollback and safe retries. No rate-limiter/audit subsystem is required for this phase.

## Open questions
None blocking registration. Login/logout and future security-policy expansion remain outside this story.
