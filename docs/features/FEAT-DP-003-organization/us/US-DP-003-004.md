# US-DP-003-004 — Revoke organization membership

## Story

As an **authorized membership operator**, I want to **remove a departing member’s workspace authority**, so that I can protect the workspace while retaining attributable history.

## Context and boundaries

Normal last-owner protection and durable membership/fresh-role rejoin are selected. U11/U12 select ordinary-removal eligibility, Owner-only Admin membership removal and Owner-controlled normal Owner reduction with continuity. Remaining removal delegation limits and access-change timing are open. The story does not delete a global User, terminate all sessions or choose project-owned grant consequences.

**State:** Jointly reviewed for requirements completeness, not client-approved or executed. Scenarios apply selected built-in defaults, protected-target rules and explicit delegated grants. Sources: [Workflow discovery](../../../deliverypilot-workflow-discovery.md) §2.2/§4; [Identity decisions](../../../architecture/modules/identity/design-decisions.md).

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-001 — Remove ordinary member

**Given** separately exercised Owner, Admin and explicitly delegated membership-manager actors have current removal authority and target an eligible non-Admin/non-Owner membership in A with a nonblank removal reason of at most 1,000 characters.

**When** the membership removal commits.

**Then** its membership/organization-role authority ends, while its stable relationship and attributable removal history remain.

### AC-002 — Unauthorized removal

**Given** a caller lacks removal authority in A.

**When** they attempt removal.

**Then** the membership and grants do not change.

### AC-003 — Historical attribution

**Given** a removed member authored prior approval/audit records and an authorized reader inspects permitted history.

**When** those records are retrieved.

**Then** their actor attribution remains and historical grants are not displayed as current authority.

### AC-004 — Last active Owner

**Given** A has one active eligible Owner and an otherwise valid removal/demotion request, including its required reason.

**When** normal administration attempts to remove or demote that Owner.

**Then** the operation cannot leave A without an active eligible Owner.

### AC-005 — Concurrent Owner reductions

**Given** A has two active eligible Owners and concurrent normal operations would remove both.

**When** the changes compete.

**Then** they cannot both commit an ownerless state; at least one active eligible Owner remains.

### AC-006 — Independent organizations

**Given** the departing person also belongs to B.

**When** their A membership is removed.

**Then** B’s relationship and the global identity are not removed by this A action.

### AC-007 — Admin membership target protection

**Given** separately exercised Admin and delegated membership-manager actors lack Owner responsibility in A, and the target holds Admin.

**When** either requests removal of the target membership.

**Then** removal is denied; only a current Owner may remove that Admin membership.

### AC-008 — Ordinary-removal target ceiling

**Given** a non-Owner has removal authority but a non-Admin/non-Owner target holds an effective workspace permission the actor does not hold.

**When** the actor attempts membership removal.

**Then** removal is denied; removal authority alone cannot bypass the target-permission ceiling.

### AC-009 — Revocation admission and queued work

**Given** a removal/role reduction commits while a previously authorized read and a queued operation exist.

**When** a new decision is evaluated, the admitted read completes and queued work starts.

**Then** the new decision denies revoked powers, the admitted read may finish, and queued work must reauthorize before effects.

### AC-010 — Mutation commit recheck

**Given** a mutation was prepared with authority that has since been revoked.

**When** Identity attempts to commit the protected change.

**Then** current authority is rechecked and the revoked change cannot commit; unavailable required facts fail closed.

### AC-011 — Required audit failure

**Given** a permitted membership removal cannot persist its required success history.

**When** the protected change attempts to complete.

**Then** neither the removal nor a false successful event survives; the operation reports failure.

### AC-012 — Missing reduction reason

**Given** a membership removal has an empty or over-1,000-character reason.

**When** the actor submits removal.

**Then** validation rejects the change without mutation; no success history is fabricated.

### AC-013 — Owner removes an Admin

**Given** a current Owner supplies a valid reason to remove an Admin membership in the same workspace.

**When** the removal commits with required history.

**Then** that Admin’s membership/organization grants end with preserved attribution; no other workspace or global User is deleted.
