# US-DP-003-009 — Review membership and role history

## Story

As an **explicitly authorized audit reader**, I want to **inspect attributable access changes**, so that I can explain current and historical membership authority.

## Context and boundaries

The jointly refined retrieval capability completes the existing audit-review need. DATA-01 selects retained history; AUTHZ-07/U11 selects Owners/Admins and explicitly delegated audit readers. U20 selects audit fields/reasons and workspace-lifetime retention; exact read-code/transport representation is task design. No platform assignment is a customer-history bypass.

**State:** Jointly reviewed for requirements completeness, not client-approved or executed. Scenarios apply selected built-in defaults, protected-target rules and explicit delegated grants. Sources: [Workflow discovery](../../../deliverypilot-workflow-discovery.md) §2.2/§4; [Identity decisions](../../../architecture/modules/identity/design-decisions.md).

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-001 — Inspect recorded transitions

**Given** separately exercised Owner, Admin and delegated audit readers have current A audit-read authority, a selected permitted event field set, and a member has removal/rejoin and grant/revocation history.

**When** they inspect that history.

**Then** the recorded actors, times and actions remain distinguishable in A; historical grants are separate from current authority.

### AC-002 — Cross-workspace history denied

**Given** the reader has A authority but no B audit grant.

**When** they request B membership/grant events.

**Then** no restricted event existence, actor detail or payload is disclosed.

### AC-003 — Secrets excluded

**Given** a retained invitation event relates to a proof used for enrollment.

**When** a permitted reader inspects it.

**Then** raw links, secrets, verifiers, credentials and request dumps are absent.

### AC-004 — Read grant revoked

**Given** the reader previously had audit access but no longer has current authority.

**When** they request historical events again.

**Then** prior access does not authorize disclosure.

### AC-005 — No false success evidence

**Given** a membership change rolls back before commitment.

**When** its permitted history is inspected.

**Then** no successful committed change is fabricated from the failed operation.

### AC-006 — Audit fields and reduction reason

**Given** a permitted audit reader inspects a recorded membership removal with a nonblank reason of at most 1,000 characters.

**When** the event is retrieved.

**Then** only the selected actor/display name, scope/target IDs, action/time/outcome/revision and allowed before/after changes/reason are shown, without email fields, raw payloads or secrets.
