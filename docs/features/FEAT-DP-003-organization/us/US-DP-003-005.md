# US-DP-003-005 — Accept an invitation and join or rejoin

## Story

As an **invited person**, I want to **accept an authorized offer for my account**, so that I can join or rejoin with understood current roles.

## Context and boundaries

INV-01/04/05/06, AUTHZ-04 and MEM-01/02 establish proof, lifetime, sponsor revalidation and fresh-role invariants. U14 permits fresh-authorized removed-member rejoin; U18 explicitly defers suspension/restoration commands and denies suspended joining.

**State:** Jointly reviewed for requirements completeness, not client-approved or executed. Scenarios apply selected built-in defaults, protected-target rules and explicit delegated grants. Sources: [Workflow discovery](../../../deliverypilot-workflow-discovery.md) §2.2/§4; [Identity decisions](../../../architecture/modules/identity/design-decisions.md).

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-001 — First join

**Given** a first-time recipient is signed in with the current matching primary email, presents valid proof before its deadline, and has an unchanged ordinary-role offer whose inviter retains authority.

**When** they accept.

**Then** the offer is consumed once and the member receives exactly the authorized offered roles with attributable history.

### AC-002 — Unverified account with valid invitation proof

**Given** the matching eligible signed-in account has no global email-verification fact but holds the valid single-use link.

**When** they accept an otherwise valid offer.

**Then** no separate fresh code is required and invitation acceptance does not silently update global email verification.

### AC-003 — Missing proof or wrong account

**Given** the signed-in account has the wrong current email, or a matching account presents no valid link proof.

**When** acceptance is attempted.

**Then** no membership or grant is created.

### AC-004 — Deadline and old links

**Given** the invitation deadline has arrived, or proof has been superseded by resend/reissue.

**When** the recipient tries the old link.

**Then** no grant is made even if stored expiry cleanup has not run.

### AC-005 — Inviter authority lost

**Given** the offered permissions are unchanged but the inviter no longer has the required authority.

**When** the recipient accepts.

**Then** acceptance is blocked and a currently authorized inviter must reissue.

### AC-006 — Offer changed

**Given** an offered role’s permissions changed, including changed and later restored permissions.

**When** the recipient accepts the earlier offer.

**Then** the earlier offer cannot grant membership/roles; authorized reissue is required.

### AC-007 — Fresh-role rejoin invariant

**Given** a removed former Admin is explicitly eligible to rejoin through a newly authorized Member-only offer.

**When** they validly accept.

**Then** the same durable membership identity/history remains and no previous Admin grant is restored.

### AC-008 — Concurrent redemption

**Given** two acceptance attempts race for the same valid proof and recipient.

**When** they complete.

**Then** there is one membership relationship and no repeated grant effect; U16/U21 additionally define response replay without duplicate effects or restored access.

### AC-009 — Existing member state restriction

**Given** separate recipients have active, removed and suspended workspace memberships.

**When** a new invitation is requested for each.

**Then** the active recipient is directed to role management; only a freshly authorized removed-member rejoin offer is eligible; suspended joining remains blocked until restoration.

### AC-010 — Accepted response replay

**Given** the same recipient accepted the offer earlier.

**When** they repeat acceptance.

**Then** an already-accepted outcome exposes only currently permitted information and creates no grants; removed/suspended recipients regain no access.

### AC-011 — Preview or decline proof

**Given** a matching signed-in recipient has valid proof for a pending offer.

**When** they preview the offer and choose decline.

**Then** only that permitted offer is shown and decline closes it without membership; wrong-account or invalid-proof attempts cannot preview or decline.
