# US-DP-003-007 — Nominate and accept an additional Owner

## Story

As an **organization Owner**, I want to **nominate an existing member who must explicitly accept additional ownership**, so that workspace responsibility is shared with agreement and continuity.

## Context and boundaries

OWN-05/U9 select nomination by an existing Owner and acceptance by an existing member. Additional ownership leaves existing Owners in place; OWN-06/07/U15 now select a separate seven-day in-app nomination, active sponsor/nominee at acceptance, cancellation by any Owner, nominee decline and transfer through acceptance then separate step-down. Demotion retains other roles or assigns Member if none remain.

**State:** Jointly reviewed for requirements completeness, not client-approved or executed. Scenarios apply selected built-in defaults, protected-target rules and explicit delegated grants. Sources: [Workflow discovery](../../../deliverypilot-workflow-discovery.md) §2.2/§4; [Identity decisions](../../../architecture/modules/identity/design-decisions.md).

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-001 — Nominate without granting

**Given** an eligible existing Owner nominates an existing same-workspace member.

**When** the nomination is made.

**Then** the nominee has no additional Owner power merely from nomination.

### AC-002 — Accept additional responsibility

**Given** a valid nomination exists for an eligible member and its nominating Owner remains authorized.

**When** that nominee explicitly accepts.

**Then** the nominee becomes an additional Owner, existing Owners remain, and the change is attributable.

### AC-003 — Wrong accepting subject

**Given** a valid nomination targets Alice.

**When** Bob attempts to accept it.

**Then** Bob gains no ownership and cannot accept on Alice’s behalf.

### AC-004 — No nomination authority

**Given** a non-Owner ordinary role manager or foreign-workspace Owner attempts nomination.

**When** they nominate a member in A.

**Then** no additional ownership is granted through that act.

### AC-005 — Normal continuity

**Given** normal concurrent removal/demotion attempts would leave no active eligible Owner.

**When** they are evaluated.

**Then** an ownerless normal state cannot commit.

### AC-006 — Expired or closed nomination

**Given** separate nominations are seven days old, cancelled by a current Owner, or declined by the nominee.

**When** the nominee tries to accept.

**Then** no additional Owner assignment is granted.

### AC-007 — Eligibility recheck at acceptance

**Given** the sponsor lost active Owner authority or the nominee is no longer an active member.

**When** acceptance is attempted.

**Then** additional ownership is not granted from the old nomination.

### AC-008 — Explicit step-down after additional acceptance

**Given** Bob has accepted additional ownership and Alice separately chooses to step down with a valid required reason; separately exercise Alice with other roles and with no other role.

**When** Alice’s normal demotion commits while Bob remains an active eligible Owner.

**Then** Alice’s Owner assignment ends, other roles remain or Member is assigned if none remain, and Bob remains Owner; acceptance alone did not demote Alice.

### AC-009 — Nomination terminal race

**Given** acceptance races authorized cancellation or nominee decline for the same nomination.

**When** the valid transitions compete.

**Then** only the first valid committed transition takes effect; no later action adds another Owner grant or undoes a committed grant through nomination closure.

### AC-010 — Compromised sole Owner

**Given** the sole eligible Owner’s account is compromised/suspended.

**When** workspace authority is evaluated.

**Then** that account is denied immediately, privileged changes are blocked without an eligible Owner, no automatic promotion occurs, and ordinary reads still require valid grants; recovery is separately scoped.

### AC-011 — Duplicate or already-Owner nomination

**Given** one target already has a pending nomination and another already holds Owner.

**When** new nominations are requested.

**Then** the pending-target request conflicts and the already-Owner target gets no new nomination or grant.

### AC-012 — Owner controls another Owner reduction

**Given** a current Owner supplies a valid reason to demote another Owner while at least one active eligible Owner remains.

**When** demotion commits.

**Then** only the target Owner assignment ends, its other roles remain or Member is assigned, and protected history is recorded.
