# US-DP-003-008 — Review workspace members

## Story

As an **explicitly authorized membership reader**, I want to **inspect permitted workspace participation and roles**, so that I can understand who currently belongs without overexposing personal data.

## Context and boundaries

AUTHZ-07/U11 selects member-list/current-role reads for Owners/Admins and explicitly delegated readers; every member may inspect their own membership/roles. General member visibility is not granted to every member. DATA-04/U12 selects display name, primary email, membership state, current workspace roles and join time. U18 selects original join time, active list default and explicit removed/suspended filters; historical roles still require audit-read authority.

**State:** Jointly reviewed for requirements completeness, not client-approved or executed. Scenarios apply selected built-in defaults, protected-target rules and explicit delegated grants. Sources: [Workflow discovery](../../../deliverypilot-workflow-discovery.md) §2.2/§4; [Identity decisions](../../../architecture/modules/identity/design-decisions.md).

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-001 — Scoped inspection

**Given** separately exercised Owner, Admin and explicitly delegated member-list readers have current authority for A.

**When** they inspect A’s memberships.

**Then** A membership results contain only the selected display name, primary email, membership state, current workspace roles and join time, with no B-only membership details.

### AC-002 — No member-read authority

**Given** an actor can select A but has no membership-read grant.

**When** they request its member list or a restricted membership.

**Then** restricted membership existence and details are not disclosed.

### AC-003 — Invitation is not membership

**Given** a recipient has a pending offer and no established membership.

**When** a permitted participation view is inspected.

**Then** the pending offer is not presented as active membership.

### AC-004 — Reading grants no management

**Given** a reader lacks role-management/removal powers.

**When** they use the displayed member identity to attempt those actions.

**Then** no assignment or membership changes result.

### AC-005 — Own membership without a general list grant

**Given** an active Member has no general member-list grant.

**When** they inspect their own membership and current workspace roles.

**Then** their own membership/roles are available without disclosing another member’s details or granting general member-list access.

### AC-006 — Original join time and historical filter

**Given** a removed member later rejoins, and a permitted list reader has no audit-read grant.

**When** the reader compares active results with an explicit historical-state filter.

**Then** original join time remains unchanged; removed/suspended records are not in the default active list, and historical role grants are not exposed without audit authority.
