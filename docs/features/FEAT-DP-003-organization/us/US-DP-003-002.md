# US-DP-003-002 — Invite organization member

## Story

As an **authorized workspace inviter**, I want to **issue and manage a scoped role offer**, so that I can onboard a colleague without sharing credentials or granting access before acceptance.

## Context and boundaries

Inviter authority is action-scoped, not inferred from an Admin title. AUTHZ-01–05 and INV-01/02/04/05/06 apply. The recipient’s successful acceptance is the separate US-DP-003-005 outcome. U13/U16 select invitation management, proof-protected preview/decline and pending cancellation; U21 selects proof issuance abuse limits.

**State:** Jointly reviewed for requirements completeness, not client-approved or executed. Scenarios apply selected built-in defaults, protected-target rules and explicit delegated grants. Sources: [Workflow discovery](../../../deliverypilot-workflow-discovery.md) §2.2/§4; [Identity decisions](../../../architecture/modules/identity/design-decisions.md).

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-001 — Issue role offer

**Given** an inviter has explicit invitation and required delegation powers for A, an eligible first-time recipient, and two same-workspace ordinary roles within their held/delegable powers.

**When** they issue the invitation.

**Then** one recipient-specific pending offer is created with a seven-day deadline and both roles; no membership is granted yet.

### AC-002 — No invitation authority

**Given** a caller has no invitation authority for A.

**When** they attempt to invite a recipient.

**Then** no invitation or grant is created and no restricted membership detail is disclosed.

### AC-003 — Invalid recipient proof or expired offer

**Given** separate expired, cancelled and wrong-recipient offers are presented for acceptance.

**When** redemption is attempted.

**Then** no membership or role grant results.

### AC-004 — Concurrent duplicate offers

**Given** two inviters concurrently target A and the same normalized recipient email with different ordinary role combinations.

**When** the issue attempts complete.

**Then** at most one pending offer exists; separate offers cannot stack hidden roles.

### AC-005 — Resend unchanged offer

**Given** an authorized inviter resends a still-valid unchanged offer.

**When** new proof is issued at time T.

**Then** the old proof becomes unusable and the deadline is T plus seven days; no membership is granted by resend.

### AC-006 — Permission change requires reissue

**Given** an offered role gains or loses a permission after issue.

**When** the old offer is presented for acceptance.

**Then** it grants nothing; a currently authorized reissue is required to offer the changed permissions.

### AC-007 — Delegation ceiling

**Given** an otherwise eligible inviter attempts a foreign-workspace role or a role with powers outside their held/delegable organization set.

**When** they issue the offer.

**Then** the offer is rejected without grants or changes to the other workspace.

### AC-008 — Duplicate issue requires explicit action

**Given** an unexpired pending offer already exists for A and the normalized recipient email.

**When** another Issue is requested.

**Then** an invitation conflict is returned to the authorized operator; the existing offer is neither silently replaced nor combined, and Resend/Reissue must be chosen explicitly.

### AC-009 — Delivery failure preserves valid offer

**Given** a pending offer was committed but its email delivery failed.

**When** delivery retry is processed before expiry with the offer still pending.

**Then** the offer stays pending with visible failed/retrying status; only current proof is sent and the original deadline does not move.

### AC-010 — Closed offer requires new issue

**Given** an offer is separately expired, declined or cancelled.

**When** an operator attempts Resend.

**Then** no old offer is reopened; a fresh authorized Issue is required.

### AC-011 — Issuance abuse limits

**Given** separate authorized requests hit the 60-second pair cooldown, 10-issuance pair window or 100-issuance workspace window.

**When** Issue/Resend/Reissue is attempted, including concurrent requests.

**Then** the configured limit blocks new offer/proof issuance and deadline extension; ordinary delivery retries of current proof do not consume new issuance quota.

### AC-012 — Cancel pending without undoing membership

**Given** a current invitation manager supplies a valid cancellation reason for a pending offer; a separate offer is already accepted.

**When** they cancel each offer.

**Then** the pending offer closes and its proof cannot redeem; the accepted offer cannot be cancelled to undo membership, which needs the separately authorized membership action.
