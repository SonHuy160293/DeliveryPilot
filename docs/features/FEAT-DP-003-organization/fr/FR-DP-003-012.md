# FR-DP-003-012 — Manage pending invitations

## Requirement statement

The system shall support scoped invitation management while preserving recipient/role intent and making every superseded proof unusable.

**Origin and state:** Jointly reviewed behavioral contract for task planning. [Identity decisions](../../../architecture/modules/identity/design-decisions.md) INV-02/05/06, AUTHZ-04, U10 and [feature invitation lifecycle](../feature.md#in-scope-for-this-release). User-selected rules retain their stated scope; BA-derived scenarios elaborate those choices without claiming customer approval or implementation.

## Preconditions and trigger

An authorized inviter requests a permitted invitation-management action. The intended recipient may preview/decline only with matching current sign-in and valid proof under INV-09/U16.

## Main behavior

An explicit resend of an unchanged offer rotates its proof and restarts seven days from that issue; the old link is unusable. Changed offered permissions require reissue with current authorization, not an unnoticed change to the old offer. At most one pending invitation per workspace/normalized email survives. AUTHZ-10 and INV-09/U16 authorize invitation managers to inspect/cancel pending offers; preview/decline needs matching signed-in recipient plus valid proof. Accepted membership is not undone by invitation cancellation. Expired, declined or cancelled offers require fresh authorized Issue, not Resend. INV-08 selects first valid committed transition under races; accepted-subject replay adds no grants and discloses only currently permitted information. INV-10 preserves pending state on delivery failure, exposes delivery status and retries current valid proof without extending expiry; superseded/expired/terminal proof must not be sent.

## Validation, business rules and authorization

BR-006/008; NFR-001/002/003/004/008. INV-02/05/06, AUTHZ-04 and U10 establish uniqueness, lifetime and changed-offer behavior. Notification delivery is separate from membership acceptance. Proofs must not appear in lists or audit evidence. INV-11/U21 applies configurable rolling issuance limits: 60 seconds per workspace/normalized recipient, 10 issuances per pair in 24 hours and 100 per workspace in one hour. Issue/Resend/Reissue share counters, applied under concurrency; blocked operations create no offer/proof or deadline extension. Automatic delivery retries reuse valid current proof and do not count as issuance.

## Failure and edge cases

Acceptance racing resend/reissue/cancellation must not redeem superseded proof or create repeated grants. Expired-but-pending records do not permit late acceptance. INV-07/U14 selects duplicate-issue conflict requiring explicit Resend/Reissue. Delivery failure does not cancel an offer or grant membership; only current valid proof is eligible for automatic delivery retry.

## Verification criteria

- Resend of an unchanged authorized offer invalidates old proof and sets a seven-day deadline from the new issue.
- A permission change cannot be applied by redeeming the old offer; reissue requires current authorization.
- Concurrent resend/reissue cannot leave multiple pending offers for one workspace/recipient.
- A request without relevant management authority changes no invitation and reveals no restricted details.

Criteria reflect the selected scope and are not executed test results. Explicitly deferred capabilities below are outside this release.
