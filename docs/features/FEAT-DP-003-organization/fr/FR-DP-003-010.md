# FR-DP-003-010 — Nominate and accept additional ownership

## Requirement statement

The system shall require nomination by an existing Owner and explicit acceptance by the nominated existing member before granting additional workspace ownership.

**Origin and state:** Jointly reviewed behavioral contract for task planning. [Identity decisions](../../../architecture/modules/identity/design-decisions.md) OWN-01/04/05 and U9 (2026-11-03 clarification: additional Owner only). User-selected rules retain their stated scope; BA-derived scenarios elaborate those choices without claiming customer approval or implementation.

## Preconditions and trigger

A currently eligible Owner nominates a member of the same workspace; the nominated member separately accepts. Current identity, scope and eligibility must be established at each protected act.

## Main behavior

Nomination alone confers no Owner authority. Acceptance establishes additional ownership with attribution, leaving existing Owners in place. This is additional ownership, not implicit transfer/demotion. AUTHZ-08/U12 separately permits an Owner to demote/remove another Owner or step down only while an active eligible Owner remains. Normal operations must preserve at least one active eligible Owner. OWN-06/U15 selects an in-app nomination to an active existing non-Owner member, with a seven-day deadline and one pending nomination per workspace/member. Acceptance requires the sponsor still be an active Owner and the nominee still active. Any current Owner may cancel; the nominee may decline. At/after the deadline or after cancellation/decline, acceptance grants nothing. OWN-07/U15 selects transfer as additional-Owner acceptance followed by a separate step-down. Demotion retains other roles or assigns Member if none remain; removal ends all workspace grants.

## Validation, business rules and authorization

BR-006/008 and NFR-001/002/003/004. OWN-01 and OWN-05/U9 establish multiple ownership and nomination/acceptance. Ordinary role managers cannot bypass protected ownership through a custom role or invitation. OWN-06 selects an in-app offer; storage/transaction representation is technical task design. REPLAY-01/U21 rejects duplicate pending nominations and creates no nomination for an already-Owner target.

## Failure and edge cases

Deny non-Owner nomination, another workspace’s nominee, or acceptance by another subject. Concurrent normal ownership reductions cannot orphan the workspace. Nomination has its own independently selected seven-day lifetime; no membership-invitation proof or automatic lifetime restart is implied.

## Verification criteria

- Nomination leaves the target’s current authority unchanged until explicit acceptance.
- An eligible nominee accepting a valid same-workspace nomination becomes an additional Owner; existing Owners remain.
- A different subject cannot accept the nomination.
- Normal membership/role changes cannot remove the last active eligible Owner, including under concurrency.

Criteria reflect the selected scope and are not executed test results. Explicitly deferred capabilities below are outside this release.
