# FR-DP-003-007 — Prevent implicit project authorization

## Requirement statement

The system shall not infer project/resource access from organization membership, organization role, platform assignment or a job title alone.

**Origin and state:** Jointly reviewed behavioral contract for task planning. [Workflow discovery](../../../deliverypilot-workflow-discovery.md) §4/Q2/Q3/Q6; [Identity decisions](../../../architecture/modules/identity/design-decisions.md) SCOPE-04 and no-content-bypass boundary. User-selected rules retain their stated scope; BA-derived scenarios elaborate those choices without claiming customer approval or implementation.

## Preconditions and trigger

A person attempts project/resource access or workspace navigation reaches a resource-owned capability.

## Main behavior

Evaluate organization facts only for their organization scope. Project participation, guest admission, sharing and reviewer authority remain separately determined by resource owners. A project guest may have no organization membership. This boundary supplies no project invitation, wildcard Owner access or customer-content support bypass.

## Validation, business rules and authorization

BR-003/004/005/008; NFR-001/002. SCOPE-04 and workflow Q2/Q3/Q6 assign resource policy to FEAT-DP-004/015. Organization membership and project invitations are different powers.

## Failure and edge cases

Even a valid Owner/Admin or platform operator without a resource grant cannot rely on that role alone for project reads/mutations. An empty workspace membership list must not automatically erase an independent guest grant.

## Verification criteria

- A workspace Owner without project entitlement receives no project access from the Owner role alone.
- A member of A cannot use that membership to access B.
- Guest participation can be assessed without creating general workspace membership.
- Workspace invitation rights do not by themselves authorize project invitations.

Criteria reflect the selected scope and are not executed test results. Explicitly deferred capabilities below are outside this release.
