# FR-DP-003-013 — Retrieve authorized membership and role audit history

## Requirement statement

The system shall let an explicitly authorized audit reader inspect permitted membership and role history without exposing secrets or another workspace’s events.

**Origin and state:** Jointly reviewed behavioral contract for task planning. [Audit need](../ur/UR-DP-003-005.md); [Identity decisions](../../../architecture/modules/identity/design-decisions.md) DATA-01/02; proposed coverage correcting recording-only semantics. User-selected rules retain their stated scope; BA-derived scenarios elaborate those choices without claiming customer approval or implementation.

## Preconditions and trigger

A caller requests membership/role history and current audit-read authority and allowed event fields are established.

## Main behavior

**Selected readership (AUTHZ-07/U11):** Owners/Admins and explicitly delegated audit readers may inspect workspace membership/role history. Audit-read authority is separate from member-list, role-management and removal authority. **Selected fields/retention (DATA-06/07/U20):** expose actor ID/permitted display name, workspace/target IDs, action, UTC time, outcome, revision and allowlisted before/after membership/grant transitions, distinguish past grants from current authority, and preserve prior evidence on removal/rejoin. Apply current read scope to historical events. Audit creation is FR-DP-003-006; storing an event does not make its contents public. Readers may scope history by target/actor/action/time within the workspace without gaining other content. Exclude emails, raw payloads and secrets. Retain history for workspace lifetime; automated purge/erasure is deferred.

## Validation, business rules and authorization

BR-008; NFR-001/002/003/008. UR-DP-003-005 explicitly needs review, not merely storage. DATA-01 selects history; DATA-07/U20 selects workspace-lifetime retention and defers purge/erasure operations. No platform support-content bypass or export capability is implied.

## Failure and edge cases

Unauthorized/cross-workspace history requests reveal no event existence or payload. Removed readers cannot rely on former access. Automated erasure/redaction is outside this release; historical actor attribution is not silently deleted.

## Verification criteria

- A reader with explicit audit-read authority can distinguish recorded removal, rejoin and grants for the authorized scope.
- Historical grants are not represented as current permissions.
- Unauthorized users receive no restricted event/personal detail.
- Permitted results exclude raw proof, verifier, credentials and unrelated workspace events.

Criteria reflect the selected scope and are not executed test results. Explicitly deferred capabilities below are outside this release.
