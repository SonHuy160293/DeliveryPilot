# FR-DP-003-005 — Revoke membership and future access

## Requirement statement

The system shall permit currently authorized removal of an organization membership while preserving its durable identity, prior attribution and normal active-Owner continuity.

**Origin and state:** Jointly reviewed behavioral contract for task planning. [Identity decisions](../../../architecture/modules/identity/design-decisions.md) OWN-01–04, MEM-01/02, DATA-01/02 and ACCESS-01. User-selected rules retain their stated scope; BA-derived scenarios elaborate those choices without claiming customer approval or implementation.

## Preconditions and trigger

An actor with removal authority requests removal of a member in the same workspace. AUTHZ-07/U11 selects Owners/Admins and explicitly delegated membership managers as eligible for ordinary non-Owner removal. AUTHZ-08/U12 restricts Admin membership removal to Owners. Only an Owner may normally demote/remove another Owner or step down, and an active eligible Owner must remain. AUTHZ-09/U13 also makes Admin-role demotion/revocation Owner-only and requires a non-Owner removal actor to hold removal permission with all target effective workspace permissions within their own. Owner actors retain all-member authority subject to last-owner continuity. Exact code/transport representation is task design; INV-08 and REPLAY-01 specify first-valid-transition, stale/conflict and no-duplicate-effect outcomes.

## Main behavior

Apply target protection to current roles, not an outdated UI selection; a subject holding Owner is governed by Owner controls even with other roles. End the target’s organization membership authority and organization role grants without deleting prior actions or the durable relationship. Normal removal/demotion cannot leave the workspace with no active eligible Owner, including concurrent changes. A future join requires fresh authorization and must not restore old roles. Do not delete the global User, unrelated organization memberships, or infer a change to separately owned guest/project grants.

## Validation, business rules and authorization

BR-002/003/006/008 and NFR-001/002/003/004/008. MEM-01/02 and OWN-01 establish history/rejoin/continuity. BR-007 is a proposed project-removal analogue, not approval of an organization-to-project cascade. Membership revocation does not equal global session logout.

## Failure and edge cases

Unauthorized/foreign-workspace removal produces no change. Concurrent Owner changes must preserve at least one active eligible Owner; the enforcement protocol is not prescribed here. ACCESS-02/U19 denies authorization decisions begun after committed revocation, allows admitted reads to finish, requires Identity mutations to recheck before commit and queued work to reauthorize at execution, and fails closed if required facts are unavailable. OWN-08 denies compromised/suspended accounts immediately; with no eligible Owner block privileged changes, no auto-promotion, and ordinary reads remain scoped. Recovery operations are separately deferred.

## Verification criteria

- A permitted ordinary-member removal ends membership-dependent authority and retains membership identity/history.
- A sole active Owner cannot be removed through normal administration.
- Concurrent normal changes cannot commit a state with no active eligible Owner.
- Historical authorship remains attributable and memberships in other workspaces remain unchanged.

Criteria reflect the selected scope and are not executed test results. Explicitly deferred capabilities below are outside this release.
