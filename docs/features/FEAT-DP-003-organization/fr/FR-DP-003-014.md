# FR-DP-003-014 — Rejoin with durable membership and newly authorized roles

## Requirement statement

The system shall reuse a removed person’s durable workspace membership when an explicitly authorized rejoin succeeds, granting only freshly authorized roles.

**Origin and state:** Jointly reviewed behavioral contract for task planning. [Identity decisions](../../../architecture/modules/identity/design-decisions.md) MEM-01/02, AUTHZ-06, DATA-01 and user-requested coverage refinement. User-selected rules retain their stated scope; BA-derived scenarios elaborate those choices without claiming customer approval or implementation.

## Preconditions and trigger

A former member presents a newly authorized admission through an invitation meeting FR-DP-003-003; current removed-state eligibility and rejoin authority must be resolved for the fixture.

## Main behavior

Retain the same User/workspace membership identity and earlier history. Rejoin must not reactivate historical grants simply by changing membership state. Establish only the current authorized roles, preserving earlier grant periods as history. Temporary suspension/restoration is distinct and explicitly deferred in MEM-05/U18; suspended relationships remain denied.

## Validation, business rules and authorization

BR-002/006/008; NFR-001/002/003/004. MEM-01/02, AUTHZ-06 and DATA-01 select durable relationship and fresh grant history. Projects and other workspace memberships remain independently authorized.

## Failure and edge cases

An old consumed invitation cannot restore membership. A suspended relationship cannot be treated as removed to bypass restoration review. MEM-04/U14 permits newly authorized removed-member rejoin, rejects active-member invitations in favor of role management and blocks suspended joining until restoration. INV-08/U16 selects already-accepted replay with current disclosure authority. MEM-05/U18 defers suspension/restoration operations in this release; suspended states remain denied. DATA-05 preserves original join time and places rejoin transitions in history.

## Verification criteria

- For an explicitly permitted removed-member rejoin, membership identity and earlier history remain unchanged.
- A former Admin rejoining with a fresh Member offer receives no historical Admin grant.
- An old proof cannot reopen membership or restore old roles.
- Concurrent authorized rejoin cannot create competing User/workspace membership relationships.

Criteria reflect the selected scope and are not executed test results. Explicitly deferred capabilities below are outside this release.
