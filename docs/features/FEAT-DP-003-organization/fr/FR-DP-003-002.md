# FR-DP-003-002 — Issue scoped membership invitation

## Requirement statement

The system shall allow a currently authorized workspace inviter to issue one recipient-specific pending invitation containing an authorized combination of organization roles.

**Origin and state:** Jointly reviewed behavioral contract for task planning. [Workflow discovery](../../../deliverypilot-workflow-discovery.md) §4/Q2/Q5; [Identity decisions](../../../architecture/modules/identity/design-decisions.md) AUTHZ-01–05, INV-01/02/05 and U10. User-selected rules retain their stated scope; BA-derived scenarios elaborate those choices without claiming customer approval or implementation.

## Preconditions and trigger

A caller requests an invitation for one workspace and recipient email. Current workspace invitation authority, permitted role delegation and recipient eligibility must be established.

## Main behavior

Validate the workspace scope and every offered role against current delegation. An invitation is an offer, not membership. Permit at most one pending offer per workspace and normalized recipient email regardless of role combination. Use the selected single-use link proof; set its deadline seven days after issue. Offered permissions are identifiable so later changes require reissue. Ordinary offers cannot bypass protected Owner nomination or grant platform privileges.

## Validation, business rules and authorization

BR-005/006/008; NFR-001/002/003/004/008. Explicitly delegated actors are not excluded merely because their title is not Admin. Role-management authority and held, catalog-delegable permissions are required when delegating roles (AUTHZ-03/05); AUTHZ-10/U13 gives Owner/Admin invitation management defaults; delegated actors need the relevant explicit powers. The selected capability matrix is authoritative; exact code/transport names are task design.

## Failure and edge cases

Unauthorized/cross-workspace roles or out-of-ceiling permissions produce no offer. Concurrent issue attempts cannot leave two pending offers. An expired record still marked pending cannot authorize late acceptance; INV-07/U14 returns an issue conflict and requires explicit Resend/Reissue; expired pending state stops blocking a fresh authorized issue, while old proof stays invalid. Delivery failure must not be presented as membership granted; INV-09/10/U16 governs cancellation, current-proof delivery retries and visible delivery failures.

## Verification criteria

- A permitted offer records the intended recipient and role combination without creating membership.
- Two concurrent offers for the same workspace/normalized email leave at most one pending offer.
- An offer for the same address in another workspace is evaluated independently.
- Missing invitation/delegation authority and foreign-workspace roles create no offer or grant.

Criteria reflect the selected scope and are not executed test results. Explicitly deferred capabilities below are outside this release.
