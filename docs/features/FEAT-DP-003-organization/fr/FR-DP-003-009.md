# FR-DP-003-009 — Assign or remove ordinary roles within delegation limits

## Requirement statement

The system shall let authorized organization role managers maintain ordinary role assignments within the workspace and delegation policy, combining current allowed permissions across valid roles.

**Origin and state:** Jointly reviewed behavioral contract for task planning. [Identity decisions](../../../architecture/modules/identity/design-decisions.md) AUTHZ-01/03/05/06, MEM-02 and DATA-01. User-selected rules retain their stated scope; BA-derived scenarios elaborate those choices without claiming customer approval or implementation.

## Preconditions and trigger

A currently eligible role manager requests an assignment/removal for an eligible membership in the same workspace.

## Main behavior

Validate role/member scope and current management authority. For grants, the actor must hold every granted permission and each permission must be catalog-delegable. Multiple roles combine allowed permissions without lifting mandatory restrictions. Owner assignments use the protected ownership workflow; platform assignments are excluded. Revoke/regrant preserves history and uses fresh authorization, not historical entitlement.

## Validation, business rules and authorization

BR-005/006/008; NFR-001/002/003/004. AUTHZ-01/03/05/06 govern additive scope, delegation and stable grant history. AUTHZ-10/U13 selects Owner/Admin management defaults; AUTHZ-09/U13 restricts revoking/demoting Admin roles to Owners. Protected target checks override ordinary role-management authority. Delegated ordinary-role managers remain eligible within current authority; business capability and stale/conflict outcomes are selected; code/transport representation is task design.

## Failure and edge cases

Deny foreign role/member pairs, nondelegable grants, inactive target eligibility or missing current authority. An ordinary role assignment cannot confer Owner/platform power. AUTHZ-12/U17 rejects stale assignment edits for refresh/review. REPLAY-01/U21 returns a current authorized no-change outcome for unchanged repeated grants/revocations without a new grant period. After current authorization, an identical already-applied intent is the no-change case; a stale differing intent is rejected for refresh/review. A fresh regrant after revocation creates a distinct authorized period; ACCESS-02/U19 governs revocation timing.

## Verification criteria

- Two permitted roles contribute their allowed permissions only in their own workspace; overlapping permissions do not multiply authority.
- A manager cannot grant a role containing a permission outside their held/delegable set.
- Ordinary assignment cannot install Owner or platform authority.
- A revoke/regrant retains previous grant facts and requires current authority; rejoin does not restore the old assignment implicitly.

Criteria reflect the selected scope and are not executed test results. Explicitly deferred capabilities below are outside this release.
