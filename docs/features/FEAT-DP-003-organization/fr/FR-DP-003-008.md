# FR-DP-003-008 — Manage custom roles while protecting built-ins

## Requirement statement

The system shall let a currently authorized organization role manager create or edit custom role definitions within their delegation ceiling while preventing customer edits to built-in role definitions.

**Origin and state:** Jointly reviewed behavioral contract for task planning. [Identity decisions](../../../architecture/modules/identity/design-decisions.md) AUTHZ-01–05, DATA-03 and user-requested coverage refinement. User-selected rules retain their stated scope; BA-derived scenarios elaborate those choices without claiming customer approval or implementation.

## Preconditions and trigger

An Owner/Admin or explicitly delegated role manager requests a custom-role definition change; current action authority and workspace scope are established.

## Main behavior

Only registered organization-delegable permissions held by the actor may be granted, and role-management authority is separately required. Protect Owner/Admin/Member definitions from customer edits. Custom role changes affect permission meaning for their holders and invalidate pending offers when offered permissions change. Merely renaming a role is not a permission addition/removal. AUTHZ-11/U17 requires trimmed case-insensitively workspace-unique names of 1–160 characters, reserved built-in names, optional description up to 1,000 characters and a system-supplied stable identifier. Retire only after current assignments and pending invitation offers are cleared; retain history and reject new use of a retired role. Customer built-in retirement/edit remains prohibited. AUTHZ-12 requires separately reviewed built-in upgrades.

## Validation, business rules and authorization

BR-005/006/008; NFR-001/002/003/004. AUTHZ-01/02/03/04/05 and DATA-03 select additive roles, protected definitions, eligible managers and code-controlled permissions. Organization roles cannot include platform powers or decide project-resource policy.

## Failure and edge cases

Deny foreign-workspace roles, unregistered/nondelegable powers, missing management authority and self-escalating edits. Reject stale edits for operator refresh/review rather than silently merge (AUTHZ-12). A concurrent assignment/offer must prevent retirement while it remains current/pending; a retired role cannot be newly assigned/offered. Role history is retained.

## Verification criteria

- A delegated non-Admin with current management authority can create a role containing only held/delegable organization powers.
- Customer edits to Owner/Admin/Member definitions are rejected without changes.
- Adding a permission the editor does not hold, including to their own assigned custom role, is rejected.
- Adding or removing offered permissions requires invitation reissue even if a later change restores the old permission set.

Criteria reflect the selected scope and are not executed test results. Explicitly deferred capabilities below are outside this release.
