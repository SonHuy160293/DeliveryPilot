# FR-DP-003-006 — Audit membership lifecycle

## Requirement statement

The system shall record attributable, protected history for material workspace membership and role changes without recording authentication or invitation secrets.

**Origin and state:** Jointly reviewed behavioral contract for task planning. [Identity decisions](../../../architecture/modules/identity/design-decisions.md) DATA-01/02, AUTHZ-06 and proposed audit representation; [global requirements](../../../requirements.md) NFR-003/004/008. User-selected rules retain their stated scope; BA-derived scenarios elaborate those choices without claiming customer approval or implementation.

## Preconditions and trigger

A workspace creation, invitation outcome, membership transition, role-definition/grant change or protected ownership change commits under its applicable policy.

## Main behavior

Record scope, actor, target, action/outcome, time and sufficient prior/new facts to distinguish ordered changes. Rejoin or regrant must not overwrite earlier grant/revocation evidence. Retain historical attribution when current access ends. Recording evidence does not by itself authorize anyone to read it; FR-DP-003-013 governs retrieval.

## Validation, business rules and authorization

NFR-003 auditability, NFR-004 ordered integrity, NFR-001/002 scope and NFR-008 minimization. DATA-01/AUTHZ-06 select shared history and distinguish grant periods; physical schema remains the Identity design proposal. DATA-07/U20 selects workspace-lifetime membership/grant history retention; automated purge/erasure workflows are deferred.

## Failure and edge cases

Never record raw invitation links/tokens, proof verifiers, credentials, password hashes or sensitive request dumps. A rolled-back action must not appear as a successful committed change. AUDIT-01/U20 requires required success-audit persistence failure to fail the state change. Denied attempts emit minimal separate security telemetry, not a claimed durable denied-attempt audit record. DATA-06 requires nonblank reasons up to 1,000 characters for membership removal, Admin/Owner demotion/revocation, invitation cancellation and custom-role retirement; optional otherwise. Exclude emails/raw payloads/secrets from audit evidence and reasons.

## Verification criteria

- Removal/rejoin and revoke/regrant retain distinguishable prior events, actors and scope.
- Successful change history cannot attribute the operation to an unrelated member/workspace.
- Audit data contains no invitation proof or authentication secret.
- Failed/rolled-back changes are not represented as successful committed changes.

Criteria reflect the selected scope and are not executed test results. Explicitly deferred capabilities below are outside this release.
