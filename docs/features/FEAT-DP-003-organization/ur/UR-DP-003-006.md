# UR-DP-003-006 — Manage roles and delegated workspace access

## Problem and context

One fixed role does not express every organization responsibility. Scoped customization must not let managers escalate themselves or change protected role definitions.

## User need

As an **authorized organization role manager**, I need to **maintain custom roles and ordinary role assignments within my delegation authority**, so that responsibilities can be distributed without overgranting access.

## Business value and observable outcome

Permitted combinations of roles supply additive allowed permissions within one workspace; protected Owner/Admin/Member definitions cannot be customer-edited, and managers cannot grant powers they do not hold or may not delegate.

## Scope, constraints and exceptions

Owners/Admins and explicitly delegated managers are eligible subject to current authority. Owner changes use the protected workflow; platform and project roles remain outside ordinary organization grants. BR-005/006/008; NFR-001/002/003/004. U17 selects retirement only after active assignments/pending offers clear, retained history, rejected stale edits and separately reviewed template upgrades.
