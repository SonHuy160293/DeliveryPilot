# UR-DP-025-004 — See the engagements I am allowed to see

**Provenance:** Drafted 2026-11-09 with `dp-feature-engineer` from the roadmap, requirements, workflow discovery and [ADR-001](../../../architecture/adr-001-delivery-module-placement.md). Business choices marked **User-selected** were made by the user in the joint feature review on 2026-11-09 (see the decision record in [feature.md](../feature.md)). `ready` records internal completeness review for task planning, not client approval; `approval_ref` stays null and nothing here is implemented.

## Problem and context
Delivery leadership needs a portfolio of the workspace's engagements, while ordinary workspace members must not see every client relationship just because they belong to the workspace (BR-003, BR-004).

## User need
As a vendor delivery leader, I need to list and open all engagements in my workspace, and as a project team member I need to see the commercial context of my own project's engagement without its money, so that client context stays need-to-know.

## Business value and observable outcome
Read-all and manage holders see every engagement; creators see their own; participants of a linked project see a summary without money; nobody sees another workspace's engagements (NFR-001).

## Scope, constraints and exceptions
- **User-selected (OQ-025-07):** visibility tiers as stated above; guests never see engagement records.
- `organization.engagements.read-all` exists in Identity (TASK-DP-003-020); `organization.engagements.manage` was added to Identity for this feature (TASK-DP-003-022).
- Cross-engagement analytics belong to FEAT-DP-014 (V2).

## Evidence and validation
**Implemented in Identity:** read-all and manage codes. **User-selected:** visibility tiers (2026-11-09).
