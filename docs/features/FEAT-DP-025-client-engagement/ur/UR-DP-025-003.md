# UR-DP-025-003 — Name the client Product Owner and contacts for the engagement

**Provenance:** Drafted 2026-11-09 with `dp-feature-engineer` from the roadmap, requirements, workflow discovery and [ADR-001](../../../architecture/adr-001-delivery-module-placement.md). Business choices marked **User-selected** were made by the user in the joint feature review on 2026-11-09 (see the decision record in [feature.md](../feature.md)). `ready` records internal completeness review for task planning, not client approval; `approval_ref` stays null and nothing here is implemented.

## Problem and context
Scope sign-off (FEAT-DP-007) and Sprint Review acceptance (FEAT-DP-026) need a known client-side decision-maker. Vendors currently rely on email threads, and a role name alone must not grant decision authority (BR-019; glossary: the assignment grants authority).

## User need
As a vendor engagement manager, I need to record the client Product Owner and key client contacts for an engagement, so that projects under it can invite and assign the right client people.

## Business value and observable outcome
Each engagement shows at most one designated client PO and its contacts; projects offer that person as the default client-PO invitee (FEAT-DP-004).

## Scope, constraints and exceptions
- **User-selected (OQ-025-06):** the engagement names contacts with at most one designated client PO; each project assigns its own client PO from admitted guests.
- The designation is information only: no account, membership, project access or decision authority (BR-003, D-07).
- Client contact data is personal data; retention follows NFR-008.

## Evidence and validation
**User-confirmed:** V3 includes the client-side PO. **User-selected:** engagement-designation/project-assignment split (2026-11-09).
