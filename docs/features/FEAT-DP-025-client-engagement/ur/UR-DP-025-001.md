# UR-DP-025-001 — Record a client engagement for a signed fixed-price agreement

**Provenance:** Drafted 2026-11-09 with `dp-feature-engineer` from the roadmap, requirements, workflow discovery and [ADR-001](../../../architecture/adr-001-delivery-module-placement.md). Business choices marked **User-selected** were made by the user in the joint feature review on 2026-11-09 (see the decision record in [feature.md](../feature.md)). `ready` records internal completeness review for task planning, not client approval; `approval_ref` stays null and nothing here is implemented.

## Problem and context
After a fixed-price contract is signed, vendor delivery managers track the client relationship, the agreement and its commitments in spreadsheets, email and contract folders. Projects then start without a shared, attributable record of which client and agreement they serve. **Agreed direction** (V3, D1, D4, D8): DeliveryPilot starts after contract signing and supports fixed-price only.

## User need
As a vendor delivery manager, I need to record a client engagement with the client's business details and the signed fixed-price agreement reference inside my workspace, so that delivery projects start from one attributable record of the client relationship.

## Business value and observable outcome
Every client project can be traced to exactly one engagement and its agreement reference (BR-046). Stakeholders validate this by recording a real pilot engagement and confirming that its details are sufficient for LC01 initialization.

## Scope, constraints and exceptions
- Engagement is owned by exactly one vendor workspace (BR-001, ADR-001 D-07).
- Fixed-price is the only contract model (D8); no contract-type selection is offered.
- **User-selected (OQ-025-01/02):** required client display name, agreement reference and signed date; optional legal name, country, engagement name and external document link. No contract file storage in MVP.
- **User-selected (OQ-025-11):** several engagements per client are allowed; a duplicate active agreement reference only produces a warning.
- Recording the engagement does not create Identity organizations, workspace memberships or project access (BR-003, D-09).
- Out of scope: CRM, presales and proposal management, invoicing, payments and accounting (D5).

## Evidence and validation
**User-confirmed:** V3 adds a minimal Client Engagement record in MVP; D8 fixes the contract model. **User-selected:** field set (joint review 2026-11-09). No customer interview is claimed; validate with a pilot vendor's real signed agreement.
