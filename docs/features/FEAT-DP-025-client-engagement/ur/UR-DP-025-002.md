# UR-DP-025-002 — Keep the agreed contract value, effort and milestones visible and attributable

**Provenance:** Drafted 2026-11-09 with `dp-feature-engineer` from the roadmap, requirements, workflow discovery and [ADR-001](../../../architecture/adr-001-delivery-module-placement.md). Business choices marked **User-selected** were made by the user in the joint feature review on 2026-11-09 (see the decision record in [feature.md](../feature.md)). `ready` records internal completeness review for task planning, not client approval; `approval_ref` stays null and nothing here is implemented.

## Problem and context
Fixed-price delivery succeeds or fails against the agreed contract value, effort envelope and milestone dates. Today these figures live in contracts and spreadsheets, so drift is noticed late and changes are not attributable. Risk signals (FEAT-DP-001) and health (FEAT-DP-027) later need these planned values.

## User need
As a vendor engagement manager, I need to record and maintain the engagement's contract value, planned effort and milestones with an attributable history of changes, so that the team and later risk signals compare delivery against what was agreed.

## Business value and observable outcome
Planned values and milestones are recorded once per engagement, and every change shows who changed what, when and why (BR-046, NFR-003). Validation: a pilot PM can answer "what was the original milestone date and who moved it?" from the product.

## Scope, constraints and exceptions
- **User-selected (OQ-025-03/04):** one fixed-price contract value in one ISO 4217 currency and planned effort in person-days; both internal to the vendor.
- **User-selected (OQ-025-05):** milestones keep their original date; moving a date needs a reason; no Change Request gate in MVP (FEAT-DP-019 decides later coupling).
- Contract value is a commercial reference, not an invoice or accounting record (D5).
- Milestones are planning objects, not a mandatory Roadmap > Milestone > Sprint hierarchy (workflow §5.3).

## Evidence and validation
**User-confirmed:** V3 names planned budget/effort and milestones. **User-selected:** money, effort and milestone shape (2026-11-09).
