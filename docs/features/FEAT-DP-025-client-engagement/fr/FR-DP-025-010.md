# FR-DP-025-010 — Show attributable engagement change history

**Provenance:** Drafted 2026-11-09 with `dp-feature-engineer` from the roadmap, requirements, workflow discovery and [ADR-001](../../../architecture/adr-001-delivery-module-placement.md). Business choices marked **User-selected** were made by the user in the joint feature review on 2026-11-09 (see the decision record in [feature.md](../feature.md)). `ready` records internal completeness review for task planning, not client approval; `approval_ref` stays null and nothing here is implemented.

## Requirement statement
The system shall keep and show, to callers with full-detail visibility, an append-only history of engagement changes: creation, detail and agreement changes, value/effort changes, milestone changes, contact designations, state changes, link-code issuance and link decisions.

## Preconditions and trigger
An engagement visible to the caller with full detail (FR-DP-025-006).

## Main behavior
Each entry records the changed area, previous and new values where applicable, actor, UTC time and reason when supplied. Entries are never edited or deleted through product behavior. Link codes themselves are never recorded.

## Validation, business rules and authorization
- BR-046 attributable changes; NFR-003 auditability.
- Secrets, tokens and link codes never appear in history; personal data follows NFR-008.

## Failure and edge cases
- Actor later removed from the workspace: entry keeps the original attribution (BR-007 principle).

## Verification criteria
- Every change in FR-DP-025-001…009 produces exactly one history entry with actor and UTC time.
- Summary-tier participants and guests cannot read history.

Criteria are scenarios for task planning, not executed tests.

## Open questions
- Retention period of engagement history follows the NFR-008 decision (cross-feature, not blocking).
