# FR-DP-025-003 — Record contract value and planned effort

**Provenance:** Drafted 2026-11-09 with `dp-feature-engineer` from the roadmap, requirements, workflow discovery and [ADR-001](../../../architecture/adr-001-delivery-module-placement.md). Business choices marked **User-selected** were made by the user in the joint feature review on 2026-11-09 (see the decision record in [feature.md](../feature.md)). `ready` records internal completeness review for task planning, not client approval; `approval_ref` stays null and nothing here is implemented.

## Requirement statement
The system shall let a holder of `organization.engagements.manage` record and change an engagement's fixed-price contract value with its currency and its planned effort in person-days, keeping attributable history.

## Preconditions and trigger
An Active engagement; caller holds `organization.engagements.manage`.

## Main behavior
Contract value: non-negative decimal amount with at most 2 decimal places and an ISO 4217 currency code. The currency can be set or changed only while the stored amount is empty; clearing the amount and currency together is allowed. Switching to a different currency requires a subsequent request after clearing the amount. Planned effort: non-negative person-days with at most 1 decimal place. Each change records previous value, actor, UTC time and optional reason. Both values may stay empty; downstream consumers treat empty as unknown, never zero.

## Validation, business rules and authorization
- **User-selected (OQ-025-03/04):** one contract value per engagement, one currency, person-days.
- Visible only to read-all and manage holders and the engagement creator; never in the project-participant summary, never to guests (FR-DP-025-006).
- No invoicing, payment or accounting behavior (D5).

## Failure and edge cases
- Negative amount, more than 2 decimals, unknown currency: rejected.
- Changing to another currency while an amount is set: rejected; clear the amount first (both steps recorded). Clearing the amount and currency together is allowed; setting another currency and amount requires a subsequent request (user-selected clarification, 2026-11-10).

## Verification criteria
- A valid amount, currency and effort are stored and shown to authorized vendor users.
- A change keeps the previous value visible in history.
- A project participant without read-all/manage and a project guest cannot read the values.

Criteria are scenarios for task planning, not executed tests.
