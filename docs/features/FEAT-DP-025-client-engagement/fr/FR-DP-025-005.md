# FR-DP-025-005 — Designate the client Product Owner and client contacts

**Provenance:** Drafted 2026-11-09 with `dp-feature-engineer` from the roadmap, requirements, workflow discovery and [ADR-001](../../../architecture/adr-001-delivery-module-placement.md). Business choices marked **User-selected** were made by the user in the joint feature review on 2026-11-09 (see the decision record in [feature.md](../feature.md)). `ready` records internal completeness review for task planning, not client approval; `approval_ref` stays null and nothing here is implemented.

## Requirement statement
The system shall let a holder of `organization.engagements.manage` record client contacts on an engagement, with at most one contact designated as client Product Owner, without granting any of them access.

## Preconditions and trigger
An Active engagement; caller holds `organization.engagements.manage`.

## Main behavior
Each contact has name (1–200), email and contact role (client PO, sponsor or other). Designating a new client PO replaces the previous designation. Changes are attributable. FEAT-DP-004 offers the designated client PO as the default client-PO invitee; the designation creates no user, membership, guest grant or decision authority.

## Validation, business rules and authorization
- **User-selected (OQ-025-06):** at most one designated client PO per engagement; projects assign their own client PO.
- BR-003/D-07: client people never become vendor workspace members through this designation.
- Personal data minimization (NFR-008).

## Failure and edge cases
- Invalid email: rejected.
- Replacing or removing the designated PO does not change any project guest grant or project client-PO assignment.

## Verification criteria
- A designated client PO appears on the engagement; the person gains no sign-in access to anything because of it.
- Designating a second contact as client PO replaces the first and records both changes.

Criteria are scenarios for task planning, not executed tests.
