# FR-DP-025-006 — List and view engagements within authorized scope

**Provenance:** Drafted 2026-11-09 with `dp-feature-engineer` from the roadmap, requirements, workflow discovery and [ADR-001](../../../architecture/adr-001-delivery-module-placement.md). Business choices marked **User-selected** were made by the user in the joint feature review on 2026-11-09 (see the decision record in [feature.md](../feature.md)). `ready` records internal completeness review for task planning, not client approval; `approval_ref` stays null and nothing here is implemented.

## Requirement statement
The system shall return engagements of the selected workspace only to entitled callers: full detail for holders of `organization.engagements.read-all` or `organization.engagements.manage` and for the engagement's creator, a summary for participants of a project linked to the engagement, and nothing for anyone else.

## Preconditions and trigger
Authenticated caller with a current session and active membership in the selected workspace (Identity check; queries may cache it for up to 30 seconds, D-04).

## Main behavior
Full detail: all fields, contract value and effort, contacts, milestones, history, client link state, and the projects under the engagement with name, status and administrator names (no project content). Summary for linked-project participants: client display name, engagement name, agreement reference, status and milestones only. Lists are paged.

## Validation, business rules and authorization
- **User-selected (OQ-025-07):** the tiers above; guests never see engagement records (unless FEAT-DP-015 later shares parts explicitly).
- **User-selected (OQ-004-08):** project name/status/administrators only on the engagement page.
- Tenant filter first, then authorization (D-07, NFR-001).

## Failure and edge cases
- Engagement ID from another workspace or outside the caller's tier: not found, no existence leak.
- Identity unavailable: fail closed.
- Participant removed from the linked project: loses the summary on the next request.

## Verification criteria
- A read-all holder sees every engagement of the workspace and none of another workspace.
- A participant of a linked project sees the summary but not contract value, effort or contacts.
- A member with no tier gets an empty list and not-found on direct access.
- A guest cannot list or open engagements.

Criteria are scenarios for task planning, not executed tests.
