# FR-DP-025-007 — Close and reopen an engagement

**Provenance:** Drafted 2026-11-09 with `dp-feature-engineer` from the roadmap, requirements, workflow discovery and [ADR-001](../../../architecture/adr-001-delivery-module-placement.md). Business choices marked **User-selected** were made by the user in the joint feature review on 2026-11-09 (see the decision record in [feature.md](../feature.md)). `ready` records internal completeness review for task planning, not client approval; `approval_ref` stays null and nothing here is implemented.

## Requirement statement
The system shall let a holder of `organization.engagements.manage` move an engagement between Active and Closed, where a Closed engagement is read-only and cannot receive new projects.

## Preconditions and trigger
An engagement in the caller's workspace; caller holds `organization.engagements.manage`.

## Main behavior
Closing requires a reason and records actor and UTC time. A Closed engagement keeps its history and linked projects; edits to details, values, milestones, contacts and link codes are rejected; FEAT-DP-004 rejects new projects under it. Removing a confirmed client link stays allowed (FR-DP-025-009). Closing is allowed while projects under it are active and does not change their state or access. Reopening requires a reason and restores editing. No deletion in MVP.

## Validation, business rules and authorization
- **User-selected (OQ-025-09):** Active/Closed only.
- **User-selected (OQ-025-13, 2026-11-10):** link removal by either side is the only change a Closed engagement accepts besides reopening.
- Project closure belongs to FEAT-DP-030; commercial closure and final acceptance are distinct facts.

## Failure and edge cases
- Closing a Closed engagement or reopening an Active one: no change.
- Outstanding link code at closing: invalidated.

## Verification criteria
- After closing, edits and new projects are rejected; history and linked projects remain readable.
- Active projects under a closed engagement keep working.
- Reopen restores edits and records who reopened and why.

Criteria are scenarios for task planning, not executed tests.
