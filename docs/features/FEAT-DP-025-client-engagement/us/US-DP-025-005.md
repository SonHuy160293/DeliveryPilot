# US-DP-025-005 — Designate the client PO and contacts

**Provenance:** Drafted 2026-11-09 with `dp-feature-engineer` from the roadmap, requirements, workflow discovery and [ADR-001](../../../architecture/adr-001-delivery-module-placement.md). Business choices marked **User-selected** were made by the user in the joint feature review on 2026-11-09 (see the decision record in [feature.md](../feature.md)). `ready` records internal completeness review for task planning, not client approval; `approval_ref` stays null and nothing here is implemented.

## Story
As a vendor engagement manager, I want to record the client PO and key client contacts, so that projects can invite the right people.

## Context and boundaries
Designation only; admission and assignment happen in FEAT-DP-004.

## Acceptance criteria
### AC-001 — Designate PO
**Given** an Active engagement **When** I record a contact with name and email as client PO **Then** the engagement shows that designated client PO.

### AC-002 — Single designation
**Given** a designated client PO exists **When** I designate another contact as client PO **Then** the new contact replaces the old designation and both changes are in history.

### AC-003 — No implicit access
**Given** a designated client PO with a DeliveryPilot account **When** they sign in **Then** they see no engagement or project data as a result of the designation.
