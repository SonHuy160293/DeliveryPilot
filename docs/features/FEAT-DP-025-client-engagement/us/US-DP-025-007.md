# US-DP-025-007 — Close and reopen an engagement

**Provenance:** Drafted 2026-11-09 with `dp-feature-engineer` from the roadmap, requirements, workflow discovery and [ADR-001](../../../architecture/adr-001-delivery-module-placement.md). Business choices marked **User-selected** were made by the user in the joint feature review on 2026-11-09 (see the decision record in [feature.md](../feature.md)). `ready` records internal completeness review for task planning, not client approval; `approval_ref` stays null and nothing here is implemented.

## Story
As a vendor engagement manager, I want to close an engagement when its agreement is complete, so that it is protected from further edits and new projects.

## Context and boundaries
Not commercial closure or project closure (FEAT-DP-030).

## Acceptance criteria
### AC-001 — Close
**Given** an Active engagement with an active project **When** I close it with a reason **Then** it becomes read-only, the project keeps working and the closure is in history.

### AC-002 — Blocked edits
**Given** a Closed engagement **When** anyone edits it or creates a project under it **Then** the action is rejected.

### AC-003 — Reopen
**Given** a Closed engagement **When** I reopen it with a reason **Then** editing is allowed again and the reopening is attributed to me.
