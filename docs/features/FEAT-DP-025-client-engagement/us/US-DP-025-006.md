# US-DP-025-006 — Browse engagements within my scope

**Provenance:** Drafted 2026-11-09 with `dp-feature-engineer` from the roadmap, requirements, workflow discovery and [ADR-001](../../../architecture/adr-001-delivery-module-placement.md). Business choices marked **User-selected** were made by the user in the joint feature review on 2026-11-09 (see the decision record in [feature.md](../feature.md)). `ready` records internal completeness review for task planning, not client approval; `approval_ref` stays null and nothing here is implemented.

## Story
As a vendor delivery leader, I want to see all engagements of my workspace, while team members see only their project's engagement summary, so that client context stays need-to-know.

## Context and boundaries
Portfolio analytics are out of scope (FEAT-DP-014).

## Acceptance criteria
### AC-001 — Read-all
**Given** I hold `organization.engagements.read-all` in workspace A **When** I list engagements **Then** I see all engagements of workspace A with full detail and none from other workspaces.

### AC-002 — Projects on engagement page
**Given** I hold read-all and engagement E has project P where I do not participate **When** I open E **Then** I see P's name, status and administrators but cannot open P's content.

### AC-003 — Participant summary
**Given** I participate in project P under engagement E **When** I open E **Then** I see the summary without contract value, effort or contacts.

### AC-004 — No tier
**Given** I am a member with no read-all, manage, creator or participation **When** I list engagements or open one by ID **Then** I see an empty list and not-found for the ID.
