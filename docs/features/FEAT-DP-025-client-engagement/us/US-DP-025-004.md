# US-DP-025-004 — Plan and adjust engagement milestones

**Provenance:** Drafted 2026-11-09 with `dp-feature-engineer` from the roadmap, requirements, workflow discovery and [ADR-001](../../../architecture/adr-001-delivery-module-placement.md). Business choices marked **User-selected** were made by the user in the joint feature review on 2026-11-09 (see the decision record in [feature.md](../feature.md)). `ready` records internal completeness review for task planning, not client approval; `approval_ref` stays null and nothing here is implemented.

## Story
As a vendor engagement manager, I want to record milestones and move their dates with a reason, so that the original commitment and every shift stay visible.

## Context and boundaries
Supports original-versus-actual visibility (D2 principle) at engagement level.

## Acceptance criteria
### AC-001 — Add milestone
**Given** an Active engagement **When** I add a milestone with a name and target date **Then** it shows the same original and current date with status Planned.

### AC-002 — Shift date
**Given** a Planned milestone **When** I change its date with a reason **Then** the original date, new date, reason, actor and UTC time are visible.

### AC-003 — Reason required
**Given** a Planned milestone **When** I change its date without a reason **Then** the change is rejected.

### AC-004 — Terminal state
**Given** an Achieved milestone **When** I try to set it back to Planned **Then** the change is rejected.

## Dependencies and testing notes
Milestone events for Risk are out of this story.
