# UR-DP-015-002 — Decide deliberately what the client sees

**Provenance:** Drafted 2026-11-10 with `dp-feature-engineer` from the roadmap, requirements, workflow discovery and [ADR-001](../../../architecture/adr-001-delivery-module-placement.md). Business choices marked **User-selected** were made by the user on 2026-11-10 (decision record in [feature.md](../feature.md)). `ready` records internal completeness review for task planning, not client approval; `approval_ref` stays null and nothing here is implemented.

## Problem and context
Vendors keep internal assumptions, open questions and stakeholder notes next to client-facing material. Today they copy selected content into emails and documents, which drifts from the source. **Agreed direction:** content is internal by default and shared explicitly (Q12, BR-004, BR-055).

## User need
As a vendor PM, I need to share selected project items with the client's guests, withdraw them again, and see at any time what the client can currently see, so that the client works from our real data without us exposing internal material.

## Business value and observable outcome
Sharing is one explicit, attributable action per item; nothing becomes client-visible as a side effect. Validation: a pilot PM can answer "what can the client see right now, and who shared it?" from the product.

## Scope, constraints and exceptions
- **User-selected (OQ-015-01):** one common rule set for all shareable item types; first types are brief items, stakeholder entries and the engagement summary.
- **User-selected (OQ-015-05/06/08):** per-item granularity; Project Administrator and member PM share; a share reaches all guests of the project.
- Sharing grants visibility only, never review assignment or decision authority (workflow §4.3, BR-019).

## Evidence and validation
**Agreed direction:** Q12, workflow §4.3, BR-055 (proposed rule). **User-selected:** scope (2026-11-10).
