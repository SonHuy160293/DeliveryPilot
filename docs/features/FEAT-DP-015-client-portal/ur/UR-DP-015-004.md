# UR-DP-015-004 — Show the client the agreed milestones of their engagement

**Provenance:** Drafted 2026-11-10 with `dp-feature-engineer` from the roadmap, requirements, workflow discovery and [ADR-001](../../../architecture/adr-001-delivery-module-placement.md). Business choices marked **User-selected** were made by the user on 2026-11-10 (decision record in [feature.md](../feature.md)). `ready` records internal completeness review for task planning, not client approval; `approval_ref` stays null and nothing here is implemented.

## Problem and context
The engagement's milestones are the dates both sides agreed, but FEAT-DP-025 keeps engagement records away from guests because they also hold contract value, effort and contacts. Clients then track milestones in a separate document.

## User need
As a vendor PM, I need to show the client's guests the agreed milestones and the agreement they belong to, and as a client Product Owner I need to see them next to my project, so that both sides look at the same commitments.

## Business value and observable outcome
Guests of a project see the engagement's client name, engagement name, agreement reference and milestones with original and current dates once the vendor shares them, and never the commercial figures. Validation: roadmap VS-02 evidence gate ("guest sees only that engagement's shared resources").

## Scope, constraints and exceptions
- **User-selected (OQ-015-04):** shareable fields are client display name, engagement name, agreement reference and milestones; contract value, effort, contacts, legal name, country, document link, link state and history are never shareable.
- Only for projects under an engagement; internal projects have nothing to share.
- **User-selected (OQ-015-06):** the sharer is a Project Administrator or member PM who also has full-detail visibility of the engagement.

## Evidence and validation
**User-selected** (2026-11-10). FR-DP-025-006 reserves this to FEAT-DP-015. Requirements open question 11 (client-shared money and effort) stays open; this need shares none.

## Open questions
- None blocking. Milestone date-change reasons are internal history and are not shown to guests.
