# UR-DP-015-001 — See my projects and what was shared with me in one place

**Provenance:** Drafted 2026-11-10 with `dp-feature-engineer` from the roadmap, requirements, workflow discovery and [ADR-001](../../../architecture/adr-001-delivery-module-placement.md). Business choices marked **User-selected** were made by the user on 2026-11-10 (decision record in [feature.md](../feature.md)). `ready` records internal completeness review for task planning, not client approval; `approval_ref` stays null and nothing here is implemented.

## Problem and context
A client person admitted to a vendor's project needs to find that project and understand what they are looking at, without learning the vendor's tool or seeing material that was not meant for them. **Agreed direction:** restricted guests take part without workspace membership (Q3, BR-003).

## User need
As a client guest, I need one place that lists the projects I was admitted to and shows, for each, a clear overview and everything the vendor shared with me, so that I can follow and contribute to my project without asking the vendor for status documents.

## Business value and observable outcome
A guest reaches their project in one step after sign-in and can tell what is shared with them. Validation: a pilot client PO finds the Product Goal, the team and the shared items without help.

## Scope, constraints and exceptions
- **User-selected (OQ-015-02):** default view is name, purpose, status, Sprint length, Product Goal, own role and client PO.
- **User-selected (OQ-015-03):** the guest sees every active participant's name and roles, including other guests.
- A guest's role keeps its FEAT-DP-004 work permissions.
- **User-selected (OQ-015-09):** no notification when something is shared or withdrawn in MVP.
- Guests never appear as, or see, workspace members beyond the project team (BR-003).

## Evidence and validation
**Agreed direction:** Q3, workflow §4.3, product definition. **User-selected:** default view and people visibility (2026-11-10). Pilot validation is still needed.
