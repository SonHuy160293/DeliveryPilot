# FR-DP-015-005 — Let a guest read the items shared with their project

**Provenance:** Drafted 2026-11-10 with `dp-feature-engineer` from the roadmap, requirements, workflow discovery and [ADR-001](../../../architecture/adr-001-delivery-module-placement.md). Business choices marked **User-selected** were made by the user on 2026-11-10 (decision record in [feature.md](../feature.md)). `ready` records internal completeness review for task planning, not client approval; `approval_ref` stays null and nothing here is implemented.

## Requirement statement
The system shall let an admitted guest list and read the items currently shared with the guests of their project, and only those.

## Preconditions and trigger
An active guest participation; at least zero shared items.

## Main behavior
The project view shows the shared brief items, the shared stakeholder entries and, when shared, the engagement summary, each with the date it was shared. A shared item shows its current content. For a stakeholder entry the guest sees name, side, organization, responsibilities and decision areas, but not the entry's email or its link to a participant.

## Validation, business rules and authorization
- **User-selected (OQ-015-07):** live content for these types; revision-bound publication of requirements belongs to FEAT-DP-006/007 (workflow Q10).
- Reading requires both the guest participation and a current share; either ending removes access (FEAT-DP-004 revocation, FR-DP-015-004).
- Who shared an item is not shown to guests.

## Failure and edge cases
- Nothing shared: an empty section, not an error.
- An item edited after sharing shows the new content on the next request.
- Direct request for an unshared item: not found.

## Verification criteria
- A guest sees exactly the shared set; unshared items are neither listed nor counted.
- A shared stakeholder entry shows no email.
- After the vendor edits a shared brief item, the guest sees the new text.
