# FR-DP-015-008 — Show vendor participants what guests can see and the sharing history

**Provenance:** Drafted 2026-11-10 with `dp-feature-engineer` from the roadmap, requirements, workflow discovery and [ADR-001](../../../architecture/adr-001-delivery-module-placement.md). Business choices marked **User-selected** were made by the user on 2026-11-10 (decision record in [feature.md](../feature.md)). `ready` records internal completeness review for task planning, not client approval; `approval_ref` stays null and nothing here is implemented.

## Requirement statement
The system shall show vendor participants with share authority the set of items currently shared with the project's guests and the attributable history of shares and withdrawals.

## Preconditions and trigger
An active member participant with share authority (FR-DP-015-003).

## Main behavior
One view lists every currently shared item with its type, who shared it and when, together with the default view guests always have. The history lists each share and withdrawal with actor, UTC time and reason when given. Every member participant sees on an item whether it is currently shared.

## Validation, business rules and authorization
- BR-055: sharing is attributable. NFR-003.
- The full sharing history follows the project change-history readers of FEAT-DP-004 (Project Administrator and member PM, OQ-004-18).
- Guests never see this view.

## Failure and edge cases
- An item that was shared and later retired or removed appears in history only.

## Verification criteria
- After two shares and one withdrawal, the current list shows one item and the history three entries with actors.
- A Developer sees the "shared" mark on an item but not the history.
- A guest PM cannot open the view.
