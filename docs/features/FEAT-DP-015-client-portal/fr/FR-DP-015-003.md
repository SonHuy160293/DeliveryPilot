# FR-DP-015-003 — Share a project item with the project's guests

**Provenance:** Drafted 2026-11-10 with `dp-feature-engineer` from the roadmap, requirements, workflow discovery and [ADR-001](../../../architecture/adr-001-delivery-module-placement.md). Business choices marked **User-selected** were made by the user on 2026-11-10 (decision record in [feature.md](../feature.md)). `ready` records internal completeness review for task planning, not client approval; `approval_ref` stays null and nothing here is implemented.

## Requirement statement
The system shall let an authorized vendor participant mark a shareable project item as shared with the project's guests, recording who shared it and when, with every item internal until that action.

## Preconditions and trigger
The caller is an active member participant with share authority on the project; the item belongs to that project and is of a shareable type.

## Main behavior
Shareable types in this contract: each brief item (problem, objectives and success criteria, scope and exclusions, assumptions, constraints and dependencies, open questions), each stakeholder entry, and the engagement summary (FR-DP-015-006). Sharing an item makes it visible to every current and future guest of the project from their next request. Sharing an already shared item changes nothing. The action records actor and UTC time and is part of the project's history.

## Validation, business rules and authorization
- BR-004, BR-055: internal by default; sharing is explicit and attributable.
- **User-selected (OQ-015-06):** share authority is Project Administrator and member PM; guests never share, including a guest PM.
- **User-selected (OQ-015-05, OQ-015-08):** per-item granularity; audience is all guests of the project.
- The Product Goal is always visible to guests and is not a shareable item.
- Sharing grants read visibility only: no edit right, review assignment or decision authority (BR-019).

## Failure and edge cases
- Item of another project or unknown type: rejected without revealing the item.
- Empty brief item: may be shared; the guest sees it as empty.
- A retired stakeholder entry cannot be shared; retiring a shared entry withdraws it.
- Concurrent share and withdraw: one consistent final state, both recorded.

## Verification criteria
- After a PM shares "scope and exclusions", a guest Viewer sees that item and still not "assumptions".
- A guest PM and a Developer cannot share.
- The share appears in history with actor and time.
