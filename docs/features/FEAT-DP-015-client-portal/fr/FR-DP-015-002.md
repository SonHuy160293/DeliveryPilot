# FR-DP-015-002 — Show a guest the default project view

**Provenance:** Drafted 2026-11-10 with `dp-feature-engineer` from the roadmap, requirements, workflow discovery and [ADR-001](../../../architecture/adr-001-delivery-module-placement.md). Business choices marked **User-selected** were made by the user on 2026-11-10 (decision record in [feature.md](../feature.md)). `ready` records internal completeness review for task planning, not client approval; `approval_ref` stays null and nothing here is implemented.

## Requirement statement
The system shall show an admitted guest a default view of the project consisting of its name, purpose, status, Sprint length, Product Goal, the guest's own roles, the assigned client Product Owner and the active team, and nothing else unless shared or granted by the guest's role.

## Preconditions and trigger
An active guest participation on the project.

## Main behavior
Show the default fields. Show the team as every active participant's display name and project roles, vendor members and guests alike, marking who is a guest and who is the client Product Owner. Show the shared items of FR-DP-015-005. A guest holding PM or BA additionally has the brief access, and a guest holding PM the stakeholder access, that the FEAT-DP-004 role matrix gives that role.

## Validation, business rules and authorization
- **User-selected (OQ-015-02, OQ-015-03).**
- Not shown by default: the six brief items other than the Product Goal, the stakeholder register, the engagement, the change history, pending invitations, activation waiver reason, and whether the project is flagged as lacking an administrator.
- Display names come from Identity when shown and are not stored (FEAT-DP-004 design). No email addresses are shown to guests (NFR-008).

## Failure and edge cases
- Project in another workspace or without participation: not found.
- Name lookup unavailable: the team is shown without names rather than failing.
- A removed or revoked participant no longer appears in the team.

## Verification criteria
- A guest Viewer sees the default fields and the team, and does not see brief items, stakeholders or the engagement.
- A guest BA sees and edits the brief; a guest PM also sees stakeholders.
- No participant email appears in any guest response.
