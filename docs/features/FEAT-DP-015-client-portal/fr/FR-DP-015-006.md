# FR-DP-015-006 — Share the engagement summary with a project's guests

**Provenance:** Drafted 2026-11-10 with `dp-feature-engineer` from the roadmap, requirements, workflow discovery and [ADR-001](../../../architecture/adr-001-delivery-module-placement.md). Business choices marked **User-selected** were made by the user on 2026-11-10 (decision record in [feature.md](../feature.md)). `ready` records internal completeness review for task planning, not client approval; `approval_ref` stays null and nothing here is implemented.

## Requirement statement
The system shall let an authorized vendor participant share, with the guests of a project under an engagement, a summary of that engagement limited to its client display name, engagement name, agreement reference and milestones.

## Preconditions and trigger
The project has an engagement; the caller has share authority on the project and full-detail visibility of that engagement (FR-DP-025-006: read-all, manage or engagement creator).

## Main behavior
Once shared, guests of that project see the four parts. Each milestone shows name, description, original and current target date and status. Guests of other projects under the same engagement see nothing unless it is shared with their project too. The share is recorded in the project's history and is visible on the engagement to full-detail users.

## Validation, business rules and authorization
- **User-selected (OQ-015-04):** shareable fields; contract value and currency, planned effort, contacts, legal name, country, document link, client-link state and engagement history are never shown to guests.
- Milestone date-change reasons are internal and not shown.
- BR-004; ADR-001 D-07 and D-09 (a client organization link grants nothing and is unrelated to this share).
- **User-selected (OQ-015-06):** authority as stated in the preconditions.

## Failure and edge cases
- Internal project or project without engagement: nothing to share; rejected.
- Caller without full engagement detail: denied without revealing the engagement.
- Closed engagement: the summary stays readable while shared, showing the Closed status.
- Project moved to another engagement or made internal while Draft: the share is withdrawn (FR-DP-015-004).
- Retired milestones are shown with their status.

## Verification criteria
- After sharing, a guest sees client name, engagement name, agreement reference and milestones, and no amount, effort or contact.
- A guest of another project under the same engagement sees nothing.
- A PM who lacks engagement read-all, manage and creator status cannot share it.

## Open questions
- Requirements open question 11: whether any monetary or effort figure is ever client-shared. This contract shares none; not blocking.
