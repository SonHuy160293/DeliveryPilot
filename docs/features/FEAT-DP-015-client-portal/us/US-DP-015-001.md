# US-DP-015-001 — Find my projects in the portal

**Provenance:** Drafted 2026-11-10 with `dp-feature-engineer` from the roadmap, requirements, workflow discovery and [ADR-001](../../../architecture/adr-001-delivery-module-placement.md). Business choices marked **User-selected** were made by the user on 2026-11-10 (decision record in [feature.md](../feature.md)). `ready` records internal completeness review for task planning, not client approval; `approval_ref` stays null and nothing here is implemented.

## Story
As a client guest, I want to see the projects I was admitted to after signing in, so that I can open the right one without a link from the vendor.

## Context and boundaries
Builds on FEAT-DP-004 guest admission. Vendor workspace names are not shown.

## Acceptance criteria
### AC-001 — My guest projects
**Given** I am an active guest of projects P and R owned by different vendors **When** I open the portal **Then** I see P and R with their status and my roles, and no other project.

### AC-002 — Nothing yet
**Given** I have no guest participation **When** I open the portal **Then** I see an empty list.

### AC-003 — Revoked
**Given** my guest access to P was revoked **When** I open the portal **Then** P is not listed.

## Dependencies and testing notes
Needs FEAT-DP-004 guest participation (TASK-DP-004-004, 010, 011).
