# US-DP-015-007 — Review what the client can see

**Provenance:** Drafted 2026-11-10 with `dp-feature-engineer` from the roadmap, requirements, workflow discovery and [ADR-001](../../../architecture/adr-001-delivery-module-placement.md). Business choices marked **User-selected** were made by the user on 2026-11-10 (decision record in [feature.md](../feature.md)). `ready` records internal completeness review for task planning, not client approval; `approval_ref` stays null and nothing here is implemented.

## Story
As a vendor PM, I want one view of everything currently shared with the client and the history of sharing, so that I can answer what the client sees and who shared it.

## Context and boundaries
History readers follow FEAT-DP-004 OQ-004-18.

## Acceptance criteria
### AC-001 — Current view
**Given** two items are shared on P **When** I open "what the client sees" **Then** I see the default view contents and the two items with who shared each and when.

### AC-002 — History
**Given** an item was shared and withdrawn **When** I open the sharing history **Then** I see both entries with actor, time and reason.

### AC-003 — Shared mark
**Given** I am a Developer on P **When** I look at a shared brief item **Then** I see that it is shared with the client, but I cannot open the sharing history.

### AC-004 — Guests excluded
**Given** I am a guest PM on P **When** I try to open the view **Then** it is denied.
