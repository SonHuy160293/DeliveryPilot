# US-DP-015-005 — Share the engagement milestones with the client

**Provenance:** Drafted 2026-11-10 with `dp-feature-engineer` from the roadmap, requirements, workflow discovery and [ADR-001](../../../architecture/adr-001-delivery-module-placement.md). Business choices marked **User-selected** were made by the user on 2026-11-10 (decision record in [feature.md](../feature.md)). `ready` records internal completeness review for task planning, not client approval; `approval_ref` stays null and nothing here is implemented.

## Story
As a vendor PM, I want to share the engagement's agreement reference and milestones with my project's guests, so that the client sees the same agreed dates as we do.

## Context and boundaries
Fields are fixed by OQ-015-04. Money, effort and contacts are never shown.

## Acceptance criteria
### AC-001 — Share summary
**Given** I am a member PM on P with full detail of its engagement E **When** I share the engagement summary **Then** guests of P see E's client display name, engagement name, agreement reference and milestones with original and current dates.

### AC-002 — Commercial data hidden
**Given** the summary is shared **When** a guest reads it **Then** no contract value, currency, effort, contact or date-change reason is included.

### AC-003 — Other project
**Given** project Q is also under E and nothing was shared with Q **When** a guest of Q opens Q **Then** they see no engagement data.

### AC-004 — No engagement authority
**Given** I am a PM on P without read-all, manage or creator status on E **When** I try to share the summary **Then** the action is denied.

### AC-005 — Engagement changed
**Given** the summary is shared and P is still Draft **When** P is moved to another engagement **Then** the share is withdrawn.
