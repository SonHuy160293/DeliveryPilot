# US-DP-015-003 — Share brief items and stakeholders with the client

**Provenance:** Drafted 2026-11-10 with `dp-feature-engineer` from the roadmap, requirements, workflow discovery and [ADR-001](../../../architecture/adr-001-delivery-module-placement.md). Business choices marked **User-selected** were made by the user on 2026-11-10 (decision record in [feature.md](../feature.md)). `ready` records internal completeness review for task planning, not client approval; `approval_ref` stays null and nothing here is implemented.

## Story
As a vendor PM, I want to share selected brief items and stakeholder entries with the client's guests, so that they read our real project information instead of a copied document.

## Context and boundaries
Per-item sharing, PM/Administrator authority and live content are user-selected (OQ-015-05, OQ-015-06, OQ-015-07).

## Acceptance criteria
### AC-001 — Share an item
**Given** I am a member PM on P and "scope and exclusions" is internal **When** I share it **Then** every guest of P sees it on their next request and it is marked as shared for the team.

### AC-002 — Only what was shared
**Given** I shared "scope and exclusions" only **When** a guest Viewer opens P **Then** they see that item and not "assumptions" or "open questions".

### AC-003 — Stakeholder without email
**Given** I shared a stakeholder entry that has an email **When** a guest reads it **Then** they see name, side, organization, responsibilities and decision areas, and no email.

### AC-004 — No authority
**Given** I am a Developer or a guest PM on P **When** I try to share an item **Then** the action is denied.

### AC-005 — Live content
**Given** a shared brief item **When** I edit it **Then** guests see the new text on their next request.
