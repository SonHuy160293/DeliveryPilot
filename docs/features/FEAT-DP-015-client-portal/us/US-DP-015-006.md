# US-DP-015-006 — Guests cannot reach anything that was not shared

**Provenance:** Drafted 2026-11-10 with `dp-feature-engineer` from the roadmap, requirements, workflow discovery and [ADR-001](../../../architecture/adr-001-delivery-module-placement.md). Business choices marked **User-selected** were made by the user on 2026-11-10 (decision record in [feature.md](../feature.md)). `ready` records internal completeness review for task planning, not client approval; `approval_ref` stays null and nothing here is implemented.

## Story
As a vendor workspace Owner, I want guests to be unable to obtain unshared information by any route, so that I can admit client people safely.

## Context and boundaries
Security story backing BR-003, BR-004 and requirements critical journeys 2 and 9.

## Acceptance criteria
### AC-001 — Guessed identifiers
**Given** I am a guest of P **When** I request an unshared brief item, a stakeholder entry, the engagement, project Q or a project of another workspace by identifier **Then** each returns not found, identical to an unknown identifier.

### AC-002 — No counts
**Given** P has five stakeholder entries and one is shared **When** I list stakeholders **Then** I see one entry and no total that reveals the other four.

### AC-003 — Revoked guest
**Given** my guest access to P was revoked **When** I request an item that is still shared **Then** I am denied.

### AC-004 — Mentions and designations
**Given** I am named as a stakeholder or engagement contact of project Q **When** I sign in **Then** I see nothing of Q.

### AC-005 — Later item types
**Given** a later feature adds an item type without a sharing definition **When** I am a guest **Then** I see nothing of that type.

## Dependencies and testing notes
Must run on PostgreSQL-backed isolation tests; AC-005 is verified in each later feature.
