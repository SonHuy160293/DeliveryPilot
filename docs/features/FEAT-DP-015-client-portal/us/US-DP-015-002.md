# US-DP-015-002 — Open my project and see the overview, goal and team

**Provenance:** Drafted 2026-11-10 with `dp-feature-engineer` from the roadmap, requirements, workflow discovery and [ADR-001](../../../architecture/adr-001-delivery-module-placement.md). Business choices marked **User-selected** were made by the user on 2026-11-10 (decision record in [feature.md](../feature.md)). `ready` records internal completeness review for task planning, not client approval; `approval_ref` stays null and nothing here is implemented.

## Story
As a client guest, I want to open my project and see its overview, Product Goal and team, so that I understand what is being built and who is involved.

## Context and boundaries
Default view only (OQ-015-02, OQ-015-03). Shared items are US-DP-015-003 and 005.

## Acceptance criteria
### AC-001 — Default view
**Given** I am a guest Viewer on P **When** I open P **Then** I see its name, purpose, status, Sprint length, Product Goal, my roles, the client Product Owner and every active participant's name and roles.

### AC-002 — Internal content hidden
**Given** nothing has been shared **When** I open P **Then** I see no brief item other than the Product Goal, no stakeholder, no engagement and no history.

### AC-003 — No emails
**Given** I view the team **When** the list is shown **Then** no participant's email address is included.

### AC-004 — Role access kept
**Given** I am a guest BA on P **When** I open the brief **Then** I can read and edit it as the FEAT-DP-004 role allows.

## Dependencies and testing notes
Names come from Identity; test the view when the name lookup is unavailable.
