# TASK-DP-004-005 — Capture the project brief and Product Goal

## Objective and source contract
Let participants with brief-edit permission (Project Administrator, PM, BA, including guests holding PM or BA) record the six brief items and the Product Goal, with missing items visible. Sources: [FR-DP-004-003](../fr/FR-DP-004-003.md); [US-DP-004-003](../us/US-DP-004-003.md); PRJ-06. All source UR/FR/US are `ready` with `approval_ref: null`: complete for planning, not client-approved.

## Behavior and invariants
- **Project access rule (defined in [TASK-DP-004-004](TASK-DP-004-004.md), reused here):** the caller needs an `Active` participant row for `(OrganizationId, ProjectId, UserId)`; otherwise 404. A `Member` participant must also pass `IWorkspaceAuthority.RequireAsync` for the workspace, uncached for commands; a denial is answered 404 and Identity being unavailable 503. A `Guest` needs no workspace membership. Permissions are the union of the caller's active roles under the FR-DP-004-005 matrix; guests never get cadence, participant, guest, client-PO, initialization or administrator permissions. A participant who lacks the needed permission gets 403.
- Commands check access and the required permission first, then lock the `Projects` row `FOR UPDATE` and recheck the caller's participation inside the transaction, so a concurrent removal or revocation wins (FR-DP-004-010).
- Every accepted change appends exactly one `project.ProjectEvents` row in the same transaction with actor, acting organization, UTC time, reason, `Details` (previous and new values) and the resulting `ProjectVersion`. A request that changes nothing appends no event.
- Stale `expectedVersion` gives 409 and no change. The pipeline transaction commits.
- Items: problem, objectives and success criteria, scope and exclusions, assumptions, constraints and dependencies, open questions, Product Goal. Each is optional, at most 4000 characters; blank is stored as null and reported as missing.
- The brief has its own `Version`; `expectedVersion` refers to the brief, so brief edits do not conflict with team or status changes (PRJ-06). The project row is not locked for brief edits.
- Events: `brief.changed` for the six items and `brief.product-goal-changed` when the Product Goal changes, the latter carrying the previous and new text.
- Every member participant can read the brief, the missing-item list and the previous Product Goal values (OQ-004-18). A guest reads the Product Goal and its previous values; a guest reads the other six items only when holding PM or BA (FEAT-DP-015 OQ-015-02). Sharing further items with guests is FEAT-DP-015, not this task.

## API contract (only for API tasks)
- **Route base (proposed):** `api/v{version:apiVersion}/organizations/{organizationId:guid}/projects`. Not exposed through the Gateway until TASK-DP-004-017.
- **Authentication and tenant:** `[Authorize]`; user and session from `ICurrentUserProvider`; the workspace is the route `organizationId` and every query filters by it first (ADR-001 D-07).
- **Errors:** 400 validation; 401; 403 participant without the permission; 404 project of another workspace or caller not an active participant (same response); 409 conflict; 503 Identity unavailable.
- **Routes (proposed):** `GET .../projects/{projectId:guid}/brief`; `PUT .../projects/{projectId:guid}/brief`; `GET .../brief/product-goal-history`.
- **Body:** `expectedVersion` and the seven items.
- **Success:** 200 with the items, `missingItems` and `version`.
- **Errors:** 400 over-length item; 403 participant without brief-edit; 409 stale version.

## Test matrix
- **New project:** all seven items missing.
- **Update Product Goal:** new value shown; previous value in the Product Goal history.
- **Permissions:** Viewer and Developer get 403; a guest BA can edit.
- **Guest read:** a guest Viewer gets the Product Goal and none of the other six items.
- **Concurrency:** stale brief version gives 409; a brief edit and a participant change in parallel both succeed.
- **Limits:** 4001 characters gives 400.
