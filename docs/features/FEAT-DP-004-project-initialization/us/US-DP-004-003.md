## Story
As a vendor PM or BA, I want to fill in the brief and Product Goal over time, so that the team and client share one view of purpose and scope.

## Context and boundaries
Structured discovery is FEAT-DP-005 (V1).

## Acceptance criteria
### AC-001 — Gaps visible
**Given** a new Draft project **When** I open the brief **Then** every brief item except name and purpose shows as missing.

### AC-002 — Update Product Goal
**Given** a project with a Product Goal **When** I change it **Then** the new goal is shown and the previous one stays in history.

### AC-003 — No permission
**Given** I am a Viewer or Developer on the project **When** I try to edit the brief **Then** the change is rejected.
