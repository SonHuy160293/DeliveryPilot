## Story
As a project administrator, I want to make another participant administrator and step down, and as a workspace Owner I want to recover a project whose administrators all left, so that administration never silently disappears.

## Context and boundaries


## Acceptance criteria
### AC-001 — Delegate
**Given** I administer P and Bob is a member participant **When** I grant Bob Project Administrator **Then** both of us administer P.

### AC-002 — Last administrator
**Given** I am the only administrator **When** I try to step down **Then** the request is rejected.

### AC-003 — Owner recovery
**Given** all administrators of P left the workspace and Bob is still a member participant **When** a workspace Owner appoints Bob with a reason **Then** Bob administers P, the recovery is audited, and the Owner still cannot open P's content.
