# US-DP-004-007 — Accept a guest invitation and see only that project

**Provenance:** Drafted 2026-11-09 with `dp-feature-engineer` from the roadmap, requirements, workflow discovery and [ADR-001](../../../architecture/adr-001-delivery-module-placement.md). Business choices marked **User-selected** were made by the user in the joint feature review on 2026-11-09 (see the decision record in [feature.md](../feature.md)). `ready` records internal completeness review for task planning, not client approval; `approval_ref` stays null and nothing here is implemented.

## Story
As an invited client person, I want to accept the project invitation with my own account, so that I can collaborate without becoming a member of the vendor's workspace.

## Context and boundaries
Content a guest sees inside the project is further limited by role and FEAT-DP-015.

## Acceptance criteria
### AC-001 — Accept
**Given** a valid invitation for my email **When** I sign in with that email and accept **Then** I can open project P with the offered role and nothing else of the vendor's workspace.

### AC-002 — Not a member
**Given** I accepted the guest invitation **When** the vendor lists workspace members **Then** I am not listed.

### AC-003 — Wrong account
**Given** a valid invitation for my email **When** another signed-in account tries to accept it **Then** no grant is created.

### AC-004 — Expired
**Given** an invitation issued 8 days ago **When** I try to accept **Then** no grant is created.

### AC-005 — Guest cannot manage people
**Given** I am a guest holding PM **When** I try to invite another guest or add a participant **Then** the action is denied.

## Dependencies and testing notes
Requires FEAT-DP-002 registration/login for the guest.
