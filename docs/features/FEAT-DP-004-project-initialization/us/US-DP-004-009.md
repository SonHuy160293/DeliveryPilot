# US-DP-004-009 — Keep projects invisible to non-participants

**Provenance:** Drafted 2026-11-09 with `dp-feature-engineer` from the roadmap, requirements, workflow discovery and [ADR-001](../../../architecture/adr-001-delivery-module-placement.md). Business choices marked **User-selected** were made by the user in the joint feature review on 2026-11-09 (see the decision record in [feature.md](../feature.md)). `ready` records internal completeness review for task planning, not client approval; `approval_ref` stays null and nothing here is implemented.

## Story
As a vendor workspace Owner, I want projects to stay invisible to members who are not on them, so that client data is need-to-know even inside my workspace.

## Context and boundaries
Security story backing BR-003/BR-004 and requirements journeys 1–2.

## Acceptance criteria
### AC-001 — Member without participation
**Given** Dana is a workspace member not on project P **When** Dana lists projects or opens P by ID **Then** P is not listed and direct access returns not found.

### AC-002 — Engagement reader
**Given** Eve holds `organization.engagements.read-all` but is not on P **When** Eve opens P's engagement and then P **Then** she sees P's name and status on the engagement page but opening P is denied.

### AC-003 — Cross-tenant guess
**Given** project P belongs to workspace A **When** a member of workspace B requests P by ID **Then** not found is returned.

## Dependencies and testing notes
Must run on PostgreSQL-backed isolation tests.
