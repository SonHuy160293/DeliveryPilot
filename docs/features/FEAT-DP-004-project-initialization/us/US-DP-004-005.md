# US-DP-004-005 — Remove a team participant

**Provenance:** Drafted 2026-11-09 with `dp-feature-engineer` from the roadmap, requirements, workflow discovery and [ADR-001](../../../architecture/adr-001-delivery-module-placement.md). Business choices marked **User-selected** were made by the user in the joint feature review on 2026-11-09 (see the decision record in [feature.md](../feature.md)). `ready` records internal completeness review for task planning, not client approval; `approval_ref` stays null and nothing here is implemented.

## Story
As a project administrator, I want to remove a participant, so that people who leave the team stop accessing the project while their history stays intact.

## Context and boundaries
BR-007.

## Acceptance criteria
### AC-001 — Remove
**Given** Bob is a Developer on P with recorded work **When** I remove Bob **Then** Bob is denied on his next request and his past actions still show him as actor.

### AC-002 — Last administrator
**Given** I am the only administrator **When** someone tries to remove me **Then** the removal is rejected.

### AC-003 — Workspace removal
**Given** Bob's workspace membership is removed **When** Bob requests project P **Then** access is denied.

## Dependencies and testing notes
Workspace-removal case relies on the per-request Identity check; event-based cleanup is TASK-DP-003-021.
