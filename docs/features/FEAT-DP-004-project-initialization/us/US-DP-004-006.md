# US-DP-004-006 — Invite the client PO as a project guest and assign them

**Provenance:** Drafted 2026-11-09 with `dp-feature-engineer` from the roadmap, requirements, workflow discovery and [ADR-001](../../../architecture/adr-001-delivery-module-placement.md). Business choices marked **User-selected** were made by the user in the joint feature review on 2026-11-09 (see the decision record in [feature.md](../feature.md)). `ready` records internal completeness review for task planning, not client approval; `approval_ref` stays null and nothing here is implemented.

## Story
As a project administrator, I want to invite the client PO by email as a guest and assign them as client Product Owner, so that they can make client decisions in this project only.

## Context and boundaries
VS-02 step 3. The engagement's designated client PO is offered as the default invitee (FR-DP-025-005).

## Acceptance criteria
### AC-001 — Invite
**Given** I administer client project P **When** I invite the client PO's email with the Viewer role **Then** a pending invitation for P only exists and an email is requested from Notification.

### AC-002 — Assign after acceptance
**Given** the client PO accepted **When** I assign them as client Product Owner **Then** the project reports them as its client decision-maker.

### AC-003 — Assign before acceptance
**Given** an invitation is still pending **When** I try to assign the client PO **Then** the assignment is rejected.

### AC-004 — Resend rotates
**Given** a pending invitation **When** I resend it **Then** the earlier link no longer works and the new one expires 7 days later.
