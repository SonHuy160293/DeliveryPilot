# FR-DP-004-011 — Maintain the project stakeholder register

**Provenance:** Drafted 2026-11-09 with `dp-feature-engineer` from the roadmap, requirements, workflow discovery and [ADR-001](../../../architecture/adr-001-delivery-module-placement.md). Business choices marked **User-selected** were made by the user in the joint feature review on 2026-11-09 (see the decision record in [feature.md](../feature.md)). `ready` records internal completeness review for task planning, not client approval; `approval_ref` stays null and nothing here is implemented.

## Requirement statement
The system shall let participants with stakeholder-edit permission record project stakeholders with name, side (vendor or client), organization, responsibilities, decision areas, optional email and optional link to a participant or guest, without granting any access.

## Preconditions and trigger
A project; caller holds stakeholder-edit per the project role matrix in FR-DP-004-005.

## Main behavior
Add, change and retire entries with attributable history. Name 1–200, organization ≤200, responsibilities and decision areas ≤2000 each. A stakeholder may exist without a user account.

## Validation, business rules and authorization
- **User-selected (OQ-004-11):** basic register; no RACI, communication calendar or kickoff record.
- BR-004: no access from an entry; NFR-008 data minimization.

## Failure and edge cases
- Linking to someone who is not a participant or guest: rejected; record them as an unlinked stakeholder instead.

## Verification criteria
- A stakeholder without an account can be recorded with responsibilities.
- Recording a stakeholder never lets them sign in to the project.

Criteria are scenarios for task planning, not executed tests.
