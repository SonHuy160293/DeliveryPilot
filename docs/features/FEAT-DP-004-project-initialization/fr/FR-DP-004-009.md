# FR-DP-004-009 — Assign the client Product Owner on a project

**Provenance:** Drafted 2026-11-09 with `dp-feature-engineer` from the roadmap, requirements, workflow discovery and [ADR-001](../../../architecture/adr-001-delivery-module-placement.md). Business choices marked **User-selected** were made by the user in the joint feature review on 2026-11-09 (see the decision record in [feature.md](../feature.md)). `ready` records internal completeness review for task planning, not client approval; `approval_ref` stays null and nothing here is implemented.

## Requirement statement
The system shall let a Project Administrator assign exactly one admitted guest as the project's client Product Owner, making that assignment the source of client decision authority used by approval and review features.

## Preconditions and trigger
The person holds an active guest grant on the project; caller is Project Administrator.

## Main behavior
Record the assignment with actor and UTC time; assigning another guest replaces it; removal is attributable. FEAT-DP-007 and FEAT-DP-026 read the assignment. When the project has an engagement, its designated client PO (FR-DP-025-005) is offered as the default invitee, but the assignment need not match it.

## Validation, business rules and authorization
- **User-selected (OQ-004-07, OQ-025-06).**
- Glossary and BR-019: the assignment grants decision authority; visibility does not.

## Failure and edge cases
- Assigning a workspace member, a non-guest or a revoked guest: rejected.
- Revoking the guest ends the assignment.

## Verification criteria
- The assigned client PO is reported as the project's client decision-maker.
- A guest Viewer without assignment has no decision authority.
- Assigning a second guest replaces the first.

Criteria are scenarios for task planning, not executed tests.

## Open questions
- None blocking for task planning; decisions are recorded in the feature decision record.
