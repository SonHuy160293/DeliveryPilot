# FR-DP-004-008 — Invite and admit project guests

**Provenance:** Drafted 2026-11-09 with `dp-feature-engineer` from the roadmap, requirements, workflow discovery and [ADR-001](../../../architecture/adr-001-delivery-module-placement.md). Business choices marked **User-selected** were made by the user in the joint feature review on 2026-11-09 (see the decision record in [feature.md](../feature.md)). `ready` records internal completeness review for task planning, not client approval; `approval_ref` stays null and nothing here is implemented.

## Requirement statement
The system shall let a Project Administrator or member PM invite an external person by email to one project with any role except Project Administrator, and let that person accept with a signed-in account whose email matches, creating a guest grant without workspace membership.

## Preconditions and trigger
Caller holds guest permission (FR-DP-004-005). The invitee needs a DeliveryPilot account to accept (FEAT-DP-002).

## Main behavior
One pending invitation per project and normalized email. The invitation carries a single-use proof delivered by email through Notification and expires 7 days after issue. The email names the project, the offered role and the inviter's display name. Resending rotates the proof, invalidates the old one and restarts 7 days. Changing the offered role of a pending invitation requires a new invitation; roles of an admitted guest are changed directly (FR-DP-004-005). Acceptance requires the matching signed-in email, rechecks the inviter's current guest permission and fails if the acceptor is an active member of the owning workspace (members are added as participants instead). If the acceptor is no longer a workspace member but still has an active member participation on the project, acceptance ends that participation, revoking its roles attributably, and admits the person as a guest with the offered role in the same step. Accepting creates the guest grant with the offered role and records inviter, acceptor and UTC time. The invitee may decline; expiry creates nothing. Issuance limits mirror Identity's defaults per project and are configurable.

## Validation, business rules and authorization
- **User-selected (OQ-004-05, OQ-004-06).**
- **User-selected (OQ-004-15, OQ-004-17, 2026-11-10):** former-member conversion on acceptance; direct role changes after admission.
- BR-003: no workspace membership created; ADR-001 D-07.
- Proofs and tokens never appear in logs or events.
- **User-selected (OQ-004-19, 2026-11-10):** email content; the inviter's name is read from Identity at issue and not stored.

## Failure and edge cases
- Expired, rotated, revoked or used invitation: no grant.
- Signed-in email differs: no grant.
- Inviter lost guest permission before acceptance: no grant; a current authorized user must reissue.
- Acceptor is a workspace member: no grant, with guidance to be added as participant.
- Invitee already a guest: no duplicate.

## Verification criteria
- An accepted guest sees only the invited project with the offered role and is absent from the workspace member list.
- Another signed-in account cannot redeem the invitation; an 8-day-old invitation cannot be accepted.
- A guest PM cannot issue invitations.
- A former workspace member with a leftover member participation accepts an invitation and afterwards holds only the offered role as a guest; the ended member roles stay in history.

Criteria are scenarios for task planning, not executed tests.
