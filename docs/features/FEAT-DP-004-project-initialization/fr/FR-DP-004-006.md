# FR-DP-004-006 — Add, change and remove team participants

**Provenance:** Drafted 2026-11-09 with `dp-feature-engineer` from the roadmap, requirements, workflow discovery and [ADR-001](../../../architecture/adr-001-delivery-module-placement.md). Business choices marked **User-selected** were made by the user in the joint feature review on 2026-11-09 (see the decision record in [feature.md](../feature.md)). `ready` records internal completeness review for task planning, not client approval; `approval_ref` stays null and nothing here is implemented.

## Requirement statement
The system shall let a Project Administrator or member PM add current members of the owning workspace to the project with roles, change their non-administrator roles and remove them, revoking future access while preserving historical attribution.

## Preconditions and trigger
Caller holds participant-management permission (FR-DP-004-005); target is an active member of the owning workspace (checked with Identity).

## Main behavior
Adding creates a participation with roles; changes and removals record actor, UTC time and reason. Removed participants lose access on their next request; their past actions stay attributed (BR-007). When a participant is removed from the workspace, project access is denied on the next request by the Identity check; the participation record is ended once Identity access-change events exist (TASK-DP-003-021).

## Validation, business rules and authorization
- Team participants must be workspace members; non-members join only as guests (FR-DP-004-008).
- **User-selected (OQ-004-09/14).**
- Removing the last active Project Administrator is rejected (FR-DP-004-013).

## Failure and edge cases
- Target not a member of the owning workspace: rejected without revealing memberships elsewhere.
- Duplicate add: no second participation.
- Concurrent removal and role change: one consistent outcome.

## Verification criteria
- An added member sees the project; a removed member is denied on the next request and their history remains.
- A member removed from the workspace is denied project access on the next request.
- A Developer cannot add participants.

Criteria are scenarios for task planning, not executed tests.
