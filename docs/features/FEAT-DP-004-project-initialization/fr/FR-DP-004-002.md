# FR-DP-004-002 — Associate a project with at most one client engagement

**Provenance:** Drafted 2026-11-09 with `dp-feature-engineer` from the roadmap, requirements, workflow discovery and [ADR-001](../../../architecture/adr-001-delivery-module-placement.md). Business choices marked **User-selected** were made by the user in the joint feature review on 2026-11-09 (see the decision record in [feature.md](../feature.md)). `ready` records internal completeness review for task planning, not client approval; `approval_ref` stays null and nothing here is implemented.

## Requirement statement
The system shall let a client project reference exactly one Active engagement of the same workspace chosen at creation, let internal projects have none, allow changing the association only while the project is Draft, and record every change attributably.

## Preconditions and trigger
At creation: the engagement is Active, in the same workspace and visible to the creator with full detail (`organization.engagements.read-all`, `organization.engagements.manage`, or engagement creator; FR-DP-025-006). Later change: project is Draft and the caller is a Project Administrator who also holds `organization.engagements.manage`.

## Main behavior
Store the engagement reference within the Projects module (local FK allowed, D-08) and mark the project client or internal. While Draft, an eligible administrator may set, change or remove the engagement with a reason; once Active the association is fixed. While Draft, the same eligible administrator may switch the project between client and internal with a reason; switching to internal removes the engagement, and the kind is fixed once Active. Changes are attributable.

## Validation, business rules and authorization
- BR-046: at most one engagement per client project; internal projects need none.
- **User-selected (OQ-004-01/02):** rules above.
- **User-selected (OQ-004-16, 2026-11-10):** client/internal switch while Draft.

## Failure and edge cases
- Engagement in another workspace or not visible to the caller: not found, no existence leak.
- Closed engagement: rejected.
- Change attempt on an Active project, including a client/internal switch: rejected.
- Concurrent engagement close during project creation: one outcome wins consistently.

## Verification criteria
- A project created under an Active engagement shows that engagement.
- Referencing another workspace's engagement fails without leaking existence.
- An internal project has no engagement and is valid.
- After activation, changing the engagement is rejected.
- A Draft client project switched to internal has no engagement; the switch is rejected once the project is Active.

Criteria are scenarios for task planning, not executed tests.
