# FR-DP-004-010 — Revoke or leave project guest access

**Provenance:** Drafted 2026-11-09 with `dp-feature-engineer` from the roadmap, requirements, workflow discovery and [ADR-001](../../../architecture/adr-001-delivery-module-placement.md). Business choices marked **User-selected** were made by the user in the joint feature review on 2026-11-09 (see the decision record in [feature.md](../feature.md)). `ready` records internal completeness review for task planning, not client approval; `approval_ref` stays null and nothing here is implemented.

## Requirement statement
The system shall let a Project Administrator or member PM revoke a guest grant or cancel a pending invitation, and let a guest leave the project, ending future access while preserving historical decisions and attribution.

## Preconditions and trigger
Caller holds guest permission, or is the guest leaving.

## Main behavior
Revocation or leaving takes effect on the next request and records actor, UTC time and optional reason. Past decisions keep their original actor. Cancelled invitations can no longer be accepted.

## Validation, business rules and authorization
- BR-007 principle; NFR-003.
- Ending the grant of the assigned client PO ends the assignment (FR-DP-004-009).

## Failure and edge cases
- Revoking an already revoked guest: no change.
- Revocation during a guest's in-flight command: the command rechecks access before commit (D-04 pattern).

## Verification criteria
- A revoked guest is denied on the next request; their earlier decisions still show them as actor.
- A guest who leaves no longer sees the project.

Criteria are scenarios for task planning, not executed tests.
