# UR-DP-004-004 — Admit the client PO and client people as project guests

**Provenance:** Drafted 2026-11-09 with `dp-feature-engineer` from the roadmap, requirements, workflow discovery and [ADR-001](../../../architecture/adr-001-delivery-module-placement.md). Business choices marked **User-selected** were made by the user in the joint feature review on 2026-11-09 (see the decision record in [feature.md](../feature.md)). `ready` records internal completeness review for task planning, not client approval; `approval_ref` stays null and nothing here is implemented.

## Problem and context
Client POs must sign off baselines and accept increments inside the product, but they are not vendor employees and must not become workspace members or see other projects (BR-003, BR-004; requirements validation use case Agency A/Client B).

## User need
As a project administrator, I need to invite the client PO and selected client people into one project as guests with a suitable role, and as an invited client person I need to accept with my own account, so that I can collaborate on that project only.

## Business value and observable outcome
A client guest sees only the project they were admitted to, within their role; they never appear as workspace members and never manage people. Validation is VS-02's evidence gate.

## Scope, constraints and exceptions
- **User-selected (OQ-004-05):** invitation mirrors Identity invitations (single-use emailed link, matching signed-in email, 7-day expiry, rotation on resend, reissue on role change, inviter recheck at acceptance, guests may leave).
- **User-selected (OQ-004-06 + follow-up):** guests may hold any role except Project Administrator, but never participant, guest or role management, client-PO assignment, cadence or initialization powers.
- **User-selected (OQ-004-07):** exactly one client PO per project, assigned from admitted guests.
- Guest grants never require or imply vendor workspace membership (ADR-001 D-07); a client organization link grants nothing (D-09).
- What a guest sees beyond project membership (shared items, health) is FEAT-DP-015.

## Evidence and validation
**User-confirmed:** Q2/Q3, BR-003. **Accepted architecture:** D-07. **User-selected:** admission flow and guest roles (2026-11-09).
